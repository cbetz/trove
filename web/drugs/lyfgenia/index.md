# Lyfgenia (lovotibeglogene autotemcel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lyfgenia (lovotibeglogene autotemcel) on December 8, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125788; the manufacturer is bluebird bio, Inc.

## Lyfgenia approval facts

- Brand name: Lyfgenia
- Active ingredient: lovotibeglogene autotemcel
- Manufacturer: bluebird bio, Inc
- Application: BLA 125788
- FDA approval date: December 8, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Lyfgenia is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: Treatment of patients 12 years of age or older with sickle cell disease and a history of vaso-occlusive events (VOEs).
