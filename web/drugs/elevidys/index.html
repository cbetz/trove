Elevidys (delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl) FDA approval

Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc · BLA 125781 · approved Jun 22, 2023

The FDA approved Elevidys (delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl) on June 22, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125781; the manufacturer is Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc.

What Elevidys is approved for

FDA's product page for Elevidys gives the indication as: ELEVIDYS is an adeno-associated virus vector-based gene therapy indicated for the treatment of patients 4 years of age and older with Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) who are ambulatory and have a confirmed mutation in the DMD gene.Limitations of Use:ELEVIDYS is not recommended in patients with:Preexisting liver impairment (defined as gamma-glutamyl transferase [GGT] > 2 x upper limitof normal or total bilirubin > the upper limit of normal not due to Gilbert’s syndrome) or activehepatic viral infection due to the high risk of acute serious liver injury and acute liver failure.Recent…
