# Elevidys (delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl) FDA approval

The FDA approved Elevidys (delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl) on June 22, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125781; the manufacturer is Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “ELEVIDYS is an adeno-associated virus vector-based gene therapy indicated for the treatment of patients 4 years of age and older with Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) who are ambulatory and have a confirmed mutation in the DMD gene.”

## Elevidys approval facts
