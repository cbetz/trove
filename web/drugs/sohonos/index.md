# Sohonos (palovarotene) FDA approval

The FDA approved Sohonos (palovarotene) on August 16, 2023, under NDA 215559. The sponsor is Ipsen. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce the volume of new heterotopic ossification in adults and pediatric patients (aged 8 years and older for females and 10 years and older for males) with fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Sohonos. FDA received the application on March 31, 2021 and approved it 868 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Sohonos in the established pharmacologic class: Retinoid.
