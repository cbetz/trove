# Yartemlea (narsoplimab-wuug) FDA approval

The FDA approved Yartemlea (narsoplimab-wuug) on December 23, 2025, under BLA 761152. The sponsor is Omeros Corporation. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat hematopoietic stem cell transplant-associated thrombotic microangiopathy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Yartemlea. FDA received the application on November 17, 2020 and approved it 1,862 days later.
