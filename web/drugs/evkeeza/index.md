# Evkeeza (evinacumab-dgnb) FDA approval

The FDA approved Evkeeza (evinacumab-dgnb) on February 11, 2021, under BLA 761181. The sponsor is Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Evkeeza. FDA received the application on June 11, 2020 and approved it 245 days later.
