# Exxua (gepirone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Exxua (gepirone) on September 22, 2023, under NDA 021164. The sponsor is Fabre Kramer. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat major depressive disorder.”

FDA reviewed Exxua on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on May 18, 2001 and approved it 8,162 days later.

## Exxua approval facts

- Brand name: Exxua
- Active ingredient: gepirone
- Sponsor: Fabre Kramer
- Application: NDA 021164
- FDA approval date: September 22, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: May 18, 2001
- Receipt to approval: 8,162 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
