# Exblifep (cefepime, enmetazobactam) FDA approval

The FDA approved Exblifep (cefepime, enmetazobactam) on February 22, 2024, under NDA 216165. The sponsor is Orchid Pharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat complicated urinary tract infections.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Exblifep. FDA received the application on June 22, 2023 and approved it 245 days later.
