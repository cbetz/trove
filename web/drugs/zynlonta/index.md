# Zynlonta (loncastuximab tesirine-lpyl) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zynlonta (loncastuximab tesirine-lpyl) on April 23, 2021, under BLA 761196. The sponsor is ADC Therapeutics SA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat certain types of relapsed or refractory large B-cell lymphoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Zynlonta. FDA received the application on September 21, 2020 and approved it 214 days later.
