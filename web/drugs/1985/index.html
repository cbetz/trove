FDA novel drug approvals in 1985

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 1985.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 31 novel drugs in 1985: 31 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 0 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

18 of the 31 (58%) had priority review and 3 had orphan drug designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 980 days (about 32.2 months).

What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
