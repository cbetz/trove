# Ensacove (ensartinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ensacove (ensartinib) on December 18, 2024, under NDA 218171. The sponsor is Xcovery. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-small cell lung cancer.”

FDA reviewed Ensacove on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 28, 2023 and approved it 356 days later.

## Ensacove approval facts

- Brand name: Ensacove
- Active ingredient: ensartinib
- Sponsor: Xcovery
- Application: NDA 218171
- FDA approval date: December 18, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: December 28, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 356 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
