# Bimzelx (bimekizumab) FDA approval

The FDA approved Bimzelx (bimekizumab) on October 17, 2023, under BLA 761151. The sponsor is Ucb INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate to severe plaque psoriasis in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.”

FDA reviewed Bimzelx on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 15, 2020 and approved it 1,189 days later.
