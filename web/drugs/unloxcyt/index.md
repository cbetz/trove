# Unloxcyt (cosibelimab-ipdl) FDA approval

The FDA approved Unloxcyt (cosibelimab-ipdl) on December 13, 2024, under BLA 761297. The sponsor is Checkpoint Therapeutics INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma.”

FDA reviewed Unloxcyt on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on January 3, 2023 and approved it 710 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Unloxcyt in the established pharmacologic class: Programmed Death Ligand-1 Blocker.
