# Filspari (sparsentan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Filspari (sparsentan) on February 17, 2023, under NDA 216403. The sponsor is Travere. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce proteinuria in adults with primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy at risk of rapid disease progression.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Filspari. FDA received the application on March 17, 2022 and approved it 337 days later.
