# Elahere (mirvetuximab soravtansine-gynx) FDA approval

The FDA approved Elahere (mirvetuximab soravtansine-gynx) on November 14, 2022, under BLA 761310. The sponsor is Immunogen INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat patients with recurrent ovarian cancer that is resistant to platinum therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Elahere. FDA received the application on March 28, 2022 and approved it 231 days later.
