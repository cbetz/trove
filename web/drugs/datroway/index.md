# Datroway (datopotamab deruxtecan-dlnk) FDA approval

The FDA approved Datroway (datopotamab deruxtecan-dlnk) on January 17, 2025, under BLA 761394. The sponsor is Daiichi Sankyo, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable or metastatic, HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer who have received prior endocrine-based therapy and chemotherapy for unresectable or metastatic disease.”

FDA reviewed Datroway on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on January 29, 2024 and approved it 354 days later.
