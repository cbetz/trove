# Ryzneuta (efbemalenograstim alfa-vuxw) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ryzneuta (efbemalenograstim alfa-vuxw) on November 16, 2023, under BLA 761134. The sponsor is Evive Biotechnology. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat neutropenia.”

FDA reviewed Ryzneuta on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 30, 2021 and approved it 961 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Ryzneuta in the established pharmacologic class: Leukocyte Growth Factor.
