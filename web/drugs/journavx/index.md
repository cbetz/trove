# Journavx (suzetrigine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Journavx (suzetrigine) on January 30, 2025, under NDA 219209. The sponsor is Vertex Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate to severe acute pain.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Journavx. FDA received the application on May 30, 2024 and approved it 245 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Journavx in the established pharmacologic class: Sodium Channel Blocker.
