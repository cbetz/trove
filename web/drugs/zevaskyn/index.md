# Zevaskyn (prademagene zamikeracel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zevaskyn (prademagene zamikeracel) on April 28, 2025. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125807; the manufacturer is Abeona Therapeutics, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “ZEVASKYN is an autologous cell sheet-based gene therapy indicated for the treatment of wounds in adult and pediatric patients with recessive dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa (RDEB).”

## Zevaskyn approval facts

- Brand name: Zevaskyn

- Manufacturer: Abeona Therapeutics, Inc

- FDA approval date: April 28, 2025
