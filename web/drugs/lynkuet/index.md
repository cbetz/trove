# Lynkuet (elinzanetant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lynkuet (elinzanetant) on October 24, 2025, under NDA 219469. The sponsor is Bayer HealthCare Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe vasomotor symptoms due to menopause.”

FDA reviewed Lynkuet on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 26, 2024 and approved it 455 days later.
