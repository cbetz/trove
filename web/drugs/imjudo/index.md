# Imjudo (tremelimumab) FDA approval

The FDA approved Imjudo (tremelimumab) on October 21, 2022, under BLA 761289. The sponsor is Astrazeneca AB. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) and orphan drug designation for Imjudo. FDA received the application on February 23, 2022 and approved it 240 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Imjudo in the established pharmacologic class: CTLA-4-directed Blocking Antibody.
