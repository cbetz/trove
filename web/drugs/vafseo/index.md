# Vafseo (vadadustat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vafseo (vadadustat) on March 27, 2024, under NDA 215192. The sponsor is Akebia. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat anemia due to chronic kidney disease.”

FDA reviewed Vafseo on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 29, 2021 and approved it 1,094 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Vafseo in the established pharmacologic class: Hypoxia-inducible Factor Prolyl Hydroxylase Inhibitor.
