# Lynozyfic (linvoseltamab-gcpt) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lynozyfic (linvoseltamab-gcpt) on July 2, 2025, under BLA 761400. The sponsor is Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after at least four prior lines of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor, an immunomodulatory agent, and an anti CD38 monoclonal antibody.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Lynozyfic. FDA received the application on December 22, 2023 and approved it 558 days later.
