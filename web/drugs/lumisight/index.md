# Lumisight (pegulicianine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lumisight (pegulicianine) on April 17, 2024, under NDA 214511. The sponsor is Lumicell. FDA lists the approved use as: “To use as an optical imaging agent for the detection of cancerous tissue.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Lumisight. FDA received the application on March 17, 2023 and approved it 397 days later.

## Lumisight approval facts

- Brand name: Lumisight
- Active ingredient: pegulicianine
- Sponsor: Lumicell
- Application: NDA 214511
- FDA approval date: April 17, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: March 17, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 397 days
- Dosage form: Powder
- Route of administration: Intravenous
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
