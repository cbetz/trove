# Attruby (acoramidis) FDA approval

The FDA approved Attruby (acoramidis) on November 22, 2024, under NDA 216540. The sponsor is Bridgebio Pharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat cardiomyopathy of wild-type or variant transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis.”

FDA reviewed Attruby on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 29, 2023 and approved it 359 days later.

## Attruby approval facts
