# Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) on September 9, 2022, under NDA 214958. The sponsor is Bristol. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis.”

FDA reviewed Sotyktu on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 10, 2021 and approved it 364 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Sotyktu in the established pharmacologic class: Tyrosine Kinase 2 Inhibitor.
