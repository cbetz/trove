# Hovilpri (pritelivir) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hovilpri (pritelivir) on October 9, 2026, under NDA 221154. The sponsor is Aicuris Anti-infect. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with weakened immune systems who have mucocutaneous lesions caused by herpes simplex virus infection and have not responded to other therapies.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Hovilpri application. Whether Hovilpri had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
