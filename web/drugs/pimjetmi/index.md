# Pimjetmi (pimicotinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Pimjetmi (pimicotinib) on November 10, 2026, under NDA 220414. The sponsor is Emd Serono. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumors in whom surgical resection may cause worsening functional limitation or severe morbidity.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Pimjetmi application. Whether Pimjetmi had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Pimjetmi approval facts

- Brand name: Pimjetmi
