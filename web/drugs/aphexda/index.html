Aphexda (motixafortide) FDA approval

Ayrmid Pharma · NDA 217159 · approved Sep 8, 2023

The FDA approved Aphexda (motixafortide) on September 8, 2023, under NDA 217159. The sponsor is Ayrmid Pharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To use with filgrastim (G-CSF) to mobilize hematopoietic stem cells to the peripheral blood for collection and subsequent autologous transplantation in patients with multiple myeloma.”

FDA reviewed Aphexda on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 9, 2022 and approved it 364 days later.
