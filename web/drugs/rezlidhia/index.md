# Rezlidhia (olutasidenib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rezlidhia (olutasidenib) on December 1, 2022, under NDA 215814. The sponsor is Rigel Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia with a susceptible isocitrate dehydrogenase-1 (IDH1) mutation.”

FDA reviewed Rezlidhia on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on February 15, 2022 and approved it 289 days later.
