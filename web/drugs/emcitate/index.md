# Emcitate (tiratricol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Emcitate (tiratricol) on September 28, 2026, under NDA 220963. The sponsor is Rare Thyroid. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat peripheral thyrotoxicosis in patients with MCT8 deficiency.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Emcitate application. Whether Emcitate had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Emcitate approval facts

- Brand name: Emcitate
- Active ingredient: tiratricol
- Sponsor: Rare Thyroid
- Application: NDA 220963
- FDA approval date: September 28, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan flag (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
