# Voranigo (vorasidenib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Voranigo (vorasidenib) on August 6, 2024, under NDA 218784. The sponsor is Servier. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Grade 2 astrocytoma or oligodendroglioma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Voranigo. FDA received the application on December 20, 2023 and approved it 230 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Voranigo in the established pharmacologic classes: Isocitrate Dehydrogenase 1 Inhibitor and Isocitrate Dehydrogenase 2 Inhibitor.
