# Hyrnuo (sevabertinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hyrnuo (sevabertinib) on November 19, 2025, under NDA 219972. The sponsor is Bayer HealthCare Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer with tumors that have activating HER2 tyrosine kinase domain activating mutations in patients who received a systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Hyrnuo. FDA received the application on March 28, 2025 and approved it 236 days later.
