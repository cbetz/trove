# Hympavzi (marstacimab-hncq) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hympavzi (marstacimab-hncq) on October 11, 2024, under BLA 761369. The sponsor is Pfizer INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent or reduce bleeding episodes related to hemophilia A or B.”

FDA reviewed Hympavzi on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 11, 2023 and approved it 366 days later.
