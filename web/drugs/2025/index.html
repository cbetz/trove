FDA novel drug approvals in 2025

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 2025.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 46 novel drugs in 2025: 34 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 12 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

24 of the 46 (52%) had priority review, 23 had orphan drug designation, 11 were accelerated approvals, 15 had breakthrough therapy designation and 18 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 356 days (about 11.7 months).

FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) separately approved 5 cell and gene therapy products in 2025, listed in the second table.

What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
