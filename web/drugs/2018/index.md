# FDA novel drug approvals in 2018

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 59 novel drugs in 2018: 42 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 17 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

44 of the 59 (75%) had priority review, 34 had orphan drug designation, 4 were accelerated approvals, 14 had breakthrough therapy designation and 24 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 243 days (about 8.0 months).
