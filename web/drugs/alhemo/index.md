# Alhemo (concizumab-mtci) FDA approval

The FDA approved Alhemo (concizumab-mtci) on December 20, 2024, under BLA 761315. The sponsor is Novo Nordisk INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “For routine prophylaxis to prevent bleeding episodes in hemophilia A and B.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Alhemo. FDA received the application on August 24, 2022 and approved it 849 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Alhemo in the established pharmacologic class: Tissue Factor Pathway Inhibitor Antagonist.
