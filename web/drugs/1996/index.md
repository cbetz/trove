# FDA novel drug approvals in 1996

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 59 novel drugs in 1996: 51 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 8 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

21 of the 59 (36%) had priority review, 9 had orphan drug designation and 6 were accelerated approvals. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 488 days (about 16.0 months).
