# Omisirge (omidubicel-onlv) FDA approval

The FDA approved Omisirge (omidubicel-onlv) on April 17, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125738; the manufacturer is Gamida Cell Ltd.

## Omisirge approval facts

- Brand name: Omisirge
- Active ingredient: omidubicel-onlv
- Manufacturer: Gamida Cell Ltd
- Application: BLA 125738
- FDA approval date: April 17, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Omisirge is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: For the treatment of: Adults and pediatric patients 12 years and older with hematologic malignancies who are planned for umbilical cord blood transplantation following myeloablative conditioning to reduce the time to neutrophil recovery and the incidence of infection; Adults and pediatric patients 6 years and older with severe aplastic anemia (SAA) following reduced intensity conditioning
