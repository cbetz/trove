# Symvess (acellular tissue engineered vessel-tyod) FDA approval

The FDA approved Symvess (acellular tissue engineered vessel-tyod) on December 19, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125812; the manufacturer is Humacyte Global, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Indicated for use in adults as a vascular conduit for extremity arterial injury when urgent revascularization is needed to avoid imminent limb loss, and autologous vein graft is not feasible.”
