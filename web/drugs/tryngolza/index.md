# Tryngolza (olezarsen) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tryngolza (olezarsen) on December 19, 2024, under NDA 218614. The sponsor is Ionis Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat familial chylomicronemia syndrome.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Tryngolza. FDA received the application on April 19, 2024 and approved it 244 days later.
