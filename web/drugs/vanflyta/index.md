# Vanflyta (quizartinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vanflyta (quizartinib) on July 20, 2023, under NDA 216993. The sponsor is Daiichi Sankyo INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To use as part of a treatment regimen for newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia that meets certain criteria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Vanflyta. FDA received the application on August 24, 2022 and approved it 330 days later.

## Vanflyta approval facts

- Brand name: Vanflyta

- Sponsor: Daiichi Sankyo INC
- Application: NDA 216993
- FDA approval date: July 20, 2023
- Approval year: 2023

- Receipt to approval: 330 days
- Dosage form: Tablet

- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
