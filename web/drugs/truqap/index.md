# Truqap (capivasertib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Truqap (capivasertib) on November 16, 2023, under NDA 218197. The sponsor is Astrazeneca. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat breast cancer that meets certain disease criteria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Truqap. FDA received the application on March 28, 2023 and approved it 233 days later.

## Truqap approval facts

- Brand name: Truqap
- Active ingredient: capivasertib
- Sponsor: Astrazeneca
- Application: NDA 218197
- FDA approval date: November 16, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: March 28, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 233 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
