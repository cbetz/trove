# Fexinidazole (fexinidazole) FDA approval

The FDA approved Fexinidazole (fexinidazole) on July 16, 2021, under NDA 214429. The sponsor is Sanofi. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat human African trypanosomiasis caused by the parasite Trypanosoma brucei gambiense.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Fexinidazole. FDA received the application on March 12, 2020 and approved it 491 days later.
