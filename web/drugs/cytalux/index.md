# Cytalux (pafolacianine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Cytalux (pafolacianine) on November 29, 2021, under NDA 214907. The sponsor is On Target Labs. FDA lists the approved use as: “To help identify ovarian cancer lesions.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Cytalux. FDA received the application on December 29, 2020 and approved it 335 days later.

## Cytalux approval facts

- Brand name: Cytalux
- Active ingredient: pafolacianine
- Sponsor: On Target Labs
- Application: NDA 214907
- FDA approval date: November 29, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: December 29, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 335 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Injection
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
