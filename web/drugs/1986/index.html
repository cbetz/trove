FDA novel drug approvals in 1986

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 1986.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 23 novel drugs in 1986: 20 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 3 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

14 of the 23 (61%) had priority review and 3 had orphan drug designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 965 days (about 31.7 months).

List of 1986 novel drug approvals (CDER)

In order of approval. Review designation and the expedited-program columns describe each application at the time of original approval, as FDA records them in its compilation of CDER NME and new biologic approvals. What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
