# Tregzi (Allogeneic regulatory T cell immunotherapy with HSPC and T cells-vldq) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tregzi (Allogeneic regulatory T cell immunotherapy with HSPC and T cells-vldq) on July 1, 2026. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125868; the manufacturer is Orca Biosystems Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For use in matched donor hematopoietic stem cell transplantation with myeloablative preparative regimen, for hematopoietic and immunologic reconstitution and to improve chronic graft-versus-host disease (cGVHD)-free survival, in the treatment of adults with hematological malignancies.”

## Tregzi approval facts

- Brand name: Tregzi

- Manufacturer: Orca Biosystems Inc

- FDA approval date: July 1, 2026
