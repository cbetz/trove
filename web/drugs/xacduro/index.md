# Xacduro (sulbactam, durlobactam) FDA approval

The FDA approved Xacduro (sulbactam, durlobactam) on May 23, 2023, under NDA 216974. The sponsor is Entasis Therap. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat hospital-acquired bacterial pneumonia and ventilator-associated bacterial pneumonia caused by susceptible isolates of Acinetobacter baumannii-calcoaceticus complex.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Xacduro. FDA received the application on September 29, 2022 and approved it 236 days later.
