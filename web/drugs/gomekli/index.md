# Gomekli (mirdametinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Gomekli (mirdametinib) on February 11, 2025, under NDA 219379. The sponsor is SpringWorks Therapeutics, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat neurofibromatosis type 1 who have symptomatic plexiform neurofibromas not amenable to complete resection.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Gomekli. FDA received the application on June 28, 2024 and approved it 228 days later.
