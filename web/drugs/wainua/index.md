# Wainua (eplontersen) FDA approval

The FDA approved Wainua (eplontersen) on December 21, 2023, under NDA 217388. The sponsor is Astrazeneca AB. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat polyneuropathy of hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis.”

FDA reviewed Wainua on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 22, 2022 and approved it 364 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Wainua in the established pharmacologic classes: Transthyretin-directed RNA Interaction and Antisense Oligonucleotide.
