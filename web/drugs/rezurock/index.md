# Rezurock (belumosudil) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rezurock (belumosudil) on July 16, 2021, under NDA 214783. The sponsor is Kadmon Pharms LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat chronic graft-versus-host disease after failure of at least two prior lines of systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Rezurock. FDA received the application on September 30, 2020 and approved it 289 days later.
