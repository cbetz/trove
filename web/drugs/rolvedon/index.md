# Rolvedon (eflapegrastim) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rolvedon (eflapegrastim) on September 9, 2022, under BLA 761148. The sponsor is Spectrum Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To decrease the incidence of infection in patients with non-myeloid malignancies receiving myelosuppressive anti-cancer drugs associated with clinically significant incidence of febrile neutropenia.”

FDA reviewed Rolvedon on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 24, 2019 and approved it 1,051 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Rolvedon in the established pharmacologic class: Leukocyte Growth Factor.
