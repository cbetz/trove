# Voyxact (sibeprenlimab-szsi) FDA approval

The FDA approved Voyxact (sibeprenlimab-szsi) on November 25, 2025, under BLA 761434. The sponsor is Otsuka Pharmaceutical Company, Ltd. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce proteinuria in primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy in adults at risk for disease progression.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Voyxact. FDA received the application on March 28, 2025 and approved it 242 days later.
