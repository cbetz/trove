# Veozah (fezolinetant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Veozah (fezolinetant) on May 12, 2023, under NDA 216578. The sponsor is Astellas. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate to severe hot flashes caused by menopause.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) for Veozah. FDA received the application on June 22, 2022 and approved it 324 days later.
