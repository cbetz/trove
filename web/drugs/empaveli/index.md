# Empaveli (pegcetacoplan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Empaveli (pegcetacoplan) on May 14, 2021, under NDA 215014. The sponsor is Apellis Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Empaveli. FDA received the application on September 14, 2020 and approved it 242 days later.
