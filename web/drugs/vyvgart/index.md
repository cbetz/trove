# Vyvgart (efgartigimod alfa-fcab) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vyvgart (efgartigimod alfa-fcab) on December 17, 2021, under BLA 761195. The sponsor is Argenx BV. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat generalized myasthenia gravis.”

FDA reviewed Vyvgart on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 17, 2020 and approved it 365 days later.

## Vyvgart approval facts

- Brand name: Vyvgart

- Sponsor: Argenx BV

- FDA approval date: December 17, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: December 17, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 365 days
- Dosage form: Injectable

- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
