# Hernexeos (zongertinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hernexeos (zongertinib) on August 8, 2025, under NDA 219042. The sponsor is Boehringer Ingelheim Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with unresectable or metastatic non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer whose tumors have HER2 tyrosine kinase domain activating mutations, as detected by an FDA-approved test, and who have received prior systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Hernexeos. FDA received the application on December 16, 2024 and approved it 235 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Hernexeos in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
