# Iomervu (iomeprol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Iomervu (iomeprol) on November 27, 2024, under NDA 216017. The sponsor is Bracco. FDA lists the approved use as: “For use as a radiographic contrast agent.”

FDA reviewed Iomervu on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 30, 2023 and approved it 363 days later.

## Iomervu approval facts

- Brand name: Iomervu
- Active ingredient: iomeprol
- Sponsor: Bracco
- Application: NDA 216017
- FDA approval date: November 27, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: November 30, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 363 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Intravenous; Intra-Arterial
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No

- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
