# Litfulo (ritlecitinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Litfulo (ritlecitinib) on June 23, 2023, under NDA 215830. The sponsor is Pfizer. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat severely patchy hair loss.”

FDA reviewed Litfulo on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 24, 2022 and approved it 364 days later.

## Litfulo approval facts

- Brand name: Litfulo

- Sponsor: Pfizer
- Application: NDA 215830
- FDA approval date: June 23, 2023

- Dosage form: Capsule

- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
