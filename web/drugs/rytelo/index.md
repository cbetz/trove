# Rytelo (imetelstat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rytelo (imetelstat) on June 6, 2024, under NDA 217779. The sponsor is Geron. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat low- to intermediate-1 risk myelodysplastic syndromes.”

FDA reviewed Rytelo on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 16, 2023 and approved it 356 days later.

## Rytelo approval facts

- Brand name: Rytelo
- Active ingredient: imetelstat
- Sponsor: Geron
- Application: NDA 217779
- FDA approval date: June 6, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: June 16, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 356 days
- Dosage form: Powder
- Route of administration: Intravenous
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
