# Lamzede (velmanase alfa-tycv) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lamzede (velmanase alfa-tycv) on February 16, 2023, under BLA 761278. The sponsor is Chiesi Farmaceutici SPA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-central nervous system manifestations of alpha-mannosidosis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Lamzede. FDA received the application on June 17, 2022 and approved it 244 days later.
