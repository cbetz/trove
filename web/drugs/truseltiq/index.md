# Truseltiq (infigratinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Truseltiq (infigratinib) on May 28, 2021, under NDA 214622. The sponsor is Helsinn Hlthcare. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat cholangiocarcinoma whose disease meets certain criteria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Truseltiq. FDA received the application on September 29, 2020 and approved it 241 days later.

## Truseltiq approval facts

- Brand name: Truseltiq
- Active ingredient: infigratinib
- Sponsor: Helsinn Hlthcare
- Application: NDA 214622
- FDA approval date: May 28, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: September 29, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 241 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
