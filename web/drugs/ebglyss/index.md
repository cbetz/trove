# Ebglyss (lebrikizumab-lbkz) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ebglyss (lebrikizumab-lbkz) on September 13, 2024, under BLA 761306. The sponsor is Eli Lilly And CO. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis.”

FDA reviewed Ebglyss on the standard review clock and recorded fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 28, 2022 and approved it 716 days later.
