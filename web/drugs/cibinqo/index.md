# Cibinqo (abrocitinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Cibinqo (abrocitinib) on January 14, 2022, under NDA 213871. The sponsor is Pfizer. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat refractory, moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Cibinqo. FDA received the application on August 25, 2020 and approved it 507 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Cibinqo in the established pharmacologic class: Janus Kinase Inhibitor.
