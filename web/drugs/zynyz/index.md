# Zynyz (retifanlimab-dlwr) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zynyz (retifanlimab-dlwr) on March 22, 2023, under BLA 761334. The sponsor is Incyte CORP. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat metastatic or recurrent locally advanced Merkel cell carcinoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Zynyz. FDA received the application on August 8, 2022 and approved it 226 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Zynyz in the established pharmacologic class: Programmed Death Receptor-1 Blocking Antibody.
