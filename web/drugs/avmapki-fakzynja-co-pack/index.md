# Avmapki Fakzynja Co-Pack (avutometinib and defactinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Avmapki Fakzynja Co-Pack (avutometinib and defactinib) on May 8, 2025, under NDA 219616. The sponsor is Verastem, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat KRAS-mutated recurrent low-grade serous ovarian cancer (LGSOC) after prior systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Avmapki Fakzynja Co-Pack. FDA received the application on October 31, 2024 and approved it 189 days later.
