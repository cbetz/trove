# Fruzaqla (fruquintinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Fruzaqla (fruquintinib) on November 8, 2023, under NDA 217564. The sponsor is Takeda Pharms USA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat refractory, metastatic colorectal cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Fruzaqla. FDA received the application on March 30, 2023 and approved it 223 days later.

## Fruzaqla approval facts

- Brand name: Fruzaqla

- FDA approval date: November 8, 2023
