# Jesduvroq (daprodustat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Jesduvroq (daprodustat) on February 1, 2023, under NDA 216951. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat anemia caused by chronic kidney disease for adults on dialysis for at least four months.”

FDA reviewed Jesduvroq on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on February 1, 2022 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Jesduvroq in the established pharmacologic class: Hypoxia-inducible Factor Prolyl Hydroxylase Inhibitor.
