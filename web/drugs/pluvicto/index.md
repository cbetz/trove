# Pluvicto (lutetium (177Lu) vipivotide tetraxetan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Pluvicto (lutetium (177Lu) vipivotide tetraxetan) on March 23, 2022, under NDA 215833. The sponsor is Novartis. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat prostate-specific membrane antigen-positive metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer following other therapies.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Pluvicto. FDA received the application on July 29, 2021 and approved it 237 days later.
