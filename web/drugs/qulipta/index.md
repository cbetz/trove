# Qulipta (atogepant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Qulipta (atogepant) on September 28, 2021, under NDA 215206. The sponsor is Abbvie. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent episodic migraines.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) for Qulipta. FDA received the application on January 28, 2021 and approved it 243 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Qulipta in the established pharmacologic class: Calcitonin Gene-related Peptide Receptor Antagonist.
