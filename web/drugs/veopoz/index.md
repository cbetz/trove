# Veopoz (pozelimab-bbfg) FDA approval

The FDA approved Veopoz (pozelimab-bbfg) on August 18, 2023, under BLA 761339. The sponsor is Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat patients 1 year old and older with CD55-deficient protein-losing enteropathy (PLE), also known as CHAPLE disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Veopoz. FDA received the application on December 20, 2022 and approved it 241 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Veopoz in the established pharmacologic class: Complement Inhibitor.
