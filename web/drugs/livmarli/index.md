# Livmarli (maralixibat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Livmarli (maralixibat) on September 29, 2021, under NDA 214662. The sponsor is Mirum. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat cholestatic pruritus associated with Alagille syndrome.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Livmarli. FDA received the application on January 29, 2021 and approved it 243 days later.

## Livmarli approval facts

- Brand name: Livmarli

- Sponsor: Mirum
- Application: NDA 214662
- FDA approval date: September 29, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: January 29, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 243 days

- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
