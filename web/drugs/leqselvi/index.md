# Leqselvi (deuruxolitinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Leqselvi (deuruxolitinib) on July 25, 2024, under NDA 217900. The sponsor is Sun Pharm Inds INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat severe alopecia areata.”

FDA reviewed Leqselvi on the standard review clock and recorded fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 28, 2023 and approved it 363 days later.

## Leqselvi approval facts

- Brand name: Leqselvi
- Active ingredient: deuruxolitinib
- Sponsor: Sun Pharm Inds INC
- Application: NDA 217900
- FDA approval date: July 25, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: July 28, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 363 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
