# Komzifti (ziftomenib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Komzifti (ziftomenib) on November 13, 2025, under NDA 220305. The sponsor is Kura Oncology, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia with a susceptible nucleophosmin 1 mutation who have no satisfactory alternative treatment options.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Komzifti. FDA received the application on March 31, 2025 and approved it 227 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Komzifti in the established pharmacologic class: Menin Inhibitor.
