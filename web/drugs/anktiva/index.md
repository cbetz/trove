# Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln) FDA approval

The FDA approved Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln) on April 22, 2024, under BLA 761336. The sponsor is Altor Bioscience, LLC, An Indirect Wholly-owned Su. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat bladder cancer.”

FDA reviewed Anktiva on the standard review clock and recorded breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on May 23, 2022 and approved it 700 days later.

## Anktiva approval facts

- Brand name: Anktiva
- Active ingredient: nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln
- Sponsor: Altor Bioscience, LLC, An Indirect Wholly-owned Su
- Application: BLA 761336
- FDA approval date: April 22, 2024
- Approval year: 2024

- Receipt to approval: 700 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Intravesical
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
