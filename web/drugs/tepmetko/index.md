# Tepmetko (tepotinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tepmetko (tepotinib) on February 3, 2021, under NDA 214096. The sponsor is Emd Serono INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Tepmetko. FDA received the application on June 29, 2020 and approved it 219 days later.

## Tepmetko approval facts

- Brand name: Tepmetko
- Active ingredient: tepotinib
- Sponsor: Emd Serono INC
- Application: NDA 214096
- FDA approval date: February 3, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: June 29, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 219 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
