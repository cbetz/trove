# Ngenla (somatrogon-ghla) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ngenla (somatrogon-ghla) on June 27, 2023, under BLA 761184. The sponsor is Pfizer Ireland Pharmaceuticals. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat growth failure due to inadequate secretion of endogenous growth hormone.”

FDA reviewed Ngenla on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 22, 2020 and approved it 978 days later.
