# Besremi (ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft) FDA approval

The FDA approved Besremi (ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft) on November 12, 2021, under BLA 761166. The sponsor is Pharmaessentia CORP. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat polycythemia vera, a blood disease that causes the overproduction of red blood cells.”

FDA reviewed Besremi on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 13, 2020 and approved it 609 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Besremi in the established pharmacologic class: Interferon alfa-2b.
