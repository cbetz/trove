# Exdensur (depemokimab-ulaa) FDA approval

The FDA approved Exdensur (depemokimab-ulaa) on December 16, 2025, under BLA 761458. The sponsor is GlaxoSmithKline LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat severe asthma characterized by an eosinophilic phenotype as an Komziftiadd-on maintenance therapy.”

FDA reviewed Exdensur on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 16, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.
