# Zurzuvae (zuranolone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zurzuvae (zuranolone) on August 4, 2023, under NDA 217369. The sponsor is Biogen INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat postpartum depression.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Zurzuvae. FDA received the application on December 5, 2022 and approved it 242 days later.

## Zurzuvae approval facts

- Brand name: Zurzuvae
- Active ingredient: zuranolone
- Sponsor: Biogen INC
- Application: NDA 217369
- FDA approval date: August 4, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: December 5, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 242 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
