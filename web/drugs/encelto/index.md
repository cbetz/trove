# Encelto (revakinagene taroretcel-lwey) FDA approval

The FDA approved Encelto (revakinagene taroretcel-lwey) on March 5, 2025. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125798; the manufacturer is Neurotech Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For The treatment of adults with idiopathic macular telangiectasia type 2 (MacTel).”

## Encelto approval facts

- Brand name: Encelto

- Manufacturer: Neurotech Pharmaceuticals, Inc
