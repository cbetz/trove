# Ojemda (tovorafenib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ojemda (tovorafenib) on April 23, 2024, under NDA 218033. The sponsor is Day One Biopharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory pediatric low-grade glioma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Ojemda. FDA received the application on August 31, 2023 and approved it 236 days later.

## Ojemda approval facts

- Brand name: Ojemda
- Active ingredient: tovorafenib
- Sponsor: Day One Biopharms
- Application: NDA 218033
- FDA approval date: April 23, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: August 31, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 236 days
- Dosage form: Suspension; Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 3 - New Dosage Form
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
