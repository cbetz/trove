# Rezzayo (rezafungin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rezzayo (rezafungin) on March 22, 2023, under NDA 217417. The sponsor is Mundipharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat candidemia and invasive candidiasis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Rezzayo. FDA received the application on July 22, 2022 and approved it 243 days later.
