# Vivjoa (oteseconazole) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vivjoa (oteseconazole) on April 26, 2022, under NDA 215888. The sponsor is Mycovia Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce the incidence of recurrent vulvovaginal candidiasis (RVVC) in females with a history of RVVC who are not of reproductive potential.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Vivjoa. FDA received the application on May 27, 2021 and approved it 334 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Vivjoa in the established pharmacologic class: Azole Antifungal.
