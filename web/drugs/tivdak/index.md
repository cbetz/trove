# Tivdak (tisotumab vedotin-tftv) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tivdak (tisotumab vedotin-tftv) on September 20, 2021, under BLA 761208. The sponsor is Seagen. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer with disease progression on or after chemotherapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and accelerated approval for Tivdak. FDA received the application on February 10, 2021 and approved it 222 days later.
