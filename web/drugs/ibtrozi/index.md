# Ibtrozi (taletrectinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ibtrozi (taletrectinib) on June 11, 2025, under NDA 219713. The sponsor is Nuvation Bio, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Ibtrozi. FDA received the application on October 23, 2024 and approved it 231 days later.

## Ibtrozi approval facts

- Brand name: Ibtrozi

- Dosage form: Capsule

- Review designation: Priority

- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
