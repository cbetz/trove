# Amtagvi (lifileucel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Amtagvi (lifileucel) on February 16, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125773; the manufacturer is Iovance Biotherapeutics, Inc.

## Amtagvi approval facts

- Brand name: Amtagvi
- Active ingredient: lifileucel
- Manufacturer: Iovance Biotherapeutics, Inc
- Application: BLA 125773
- FDA approval date: February 16, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Amtagvi is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: Treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma previously treated with a PD-1 blocking antibody, and if BRAF V600 mutation positive, a BRAF inhibitor with or without a MEK inhibitor. This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on objective response rate (ORR). Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial(s)
