# Lupkynis (voclosporin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lupkynis (voclosporin) on January 22, 2021, under NDA 213716. The sponsor is Aurinia. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat lupus nephritis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Lupkynis. FDA received the application on May 22, 2020 and approved it 245 days later.

## Lupkynis approval facts

- Brand name: Lupkynis
- Active ingredient: voclosporin
- Sponsor: Aurinia
- Application: NDA 213716
- FDA approval date: January 22, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: May 22, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 245 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
