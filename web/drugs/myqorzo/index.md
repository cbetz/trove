# Myqorzo (aficamten) FDA approval

The FDA approved Myqorzo (aficamten) on December 19, 2025, under NDA 219083. The sponsor is Cytokinetics, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat symptomatic obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.”

FDA reviewed Myqorzo on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 26, 2024 and approved it 449 days later.

## Myqorzo approval facts

- Brand name: Myqorzo
- Active ingredient: aficamten
- Sponsor: Cytokinetics, Inc.
- Application: NDA 219083
- FDA approval date: December 19, 2025

- Receipt to approval: 449 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
