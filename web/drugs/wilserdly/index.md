# Wilserdly (giredestrant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Wilserdly (giredestrant) on November 16, 2026, under NDA 220791. The sponsor is Genentech INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with estrogen receptor-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative, estrogen receptor 1-mutated locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer whose disease has recurred during or after endocrine-based adjuvant therapy, or has progressed on at least one endocrine-based regimen in the metastatic setting.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Wilserdly application. Whether Wilserdly had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
