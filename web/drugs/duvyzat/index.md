# Duvyzat (givinostat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Duvyzat (givinostat) on March 21, 2024, under NDA 217865. The sponsor is Italfarmaco SPA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Duchenne muscular dystrophy in individuals aged 6 years and older.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Duvyzat. FDA received the application on April 21, 2023 and approved it 335 days later.

## Duvyzat approval facts

- Brand name: Duvyzat
- Active ingredient: givinostat
- Sponsor: Italfarmaco SPA
- Application: NDA 217865
- FDA approval date: March 21, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: April 21, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 335 days
- Dosage form: Suspension
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
