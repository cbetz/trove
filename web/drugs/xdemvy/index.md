# Xdemvy (lotilaner) FDA approval

The FDA approved Xdemvy (lotilaner) on July 24, 2023, under NDA 217603. The sponsor is Tarsus. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Demodex blepharitis.”

FDA reviewed Xdemvy on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on August 25, 2022 and approved it 333 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Xdemvy in the established pharmacologic class: Ectoparasiticide.
