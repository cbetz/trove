Abecma (idecabtagene vicleucel) FDA approval

Celgene Corporation, a Bristol-Myers Squibb Company · BLA 125736 · approved Mar 26, 2021

The FDA approved Abecma (idecabtagene vicleucel) on March 26, 2021. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125736; the manufacturer is Celgene Corporation, a Bristol-Myers Squibb Company.

What Abecma is approved for

FDA's product page for Abecma gives the indication as: Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after two or more prior lines of therapy including an immunomodulatory agent, a proteasome inhibitor, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody

Copied from FDA's CBER product page, which can abbreviate. The package insert linked below has the full indication.
