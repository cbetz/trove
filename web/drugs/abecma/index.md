# Abecma (idecabtagene vicleucel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Abecma (idecabtagene vicleucel) on March 26, 2021. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125736; the manufacturer is Celgene Corporation, a Bristol-Myers Squibb Company. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after two or more prior lines of therapy including an immunomodulatory agent, a proteasome inhibitor, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody.”
