# Lumvoa (veligrotug-vvze) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lumvoa (veligrotug-vvze) on June 26, 2026, under BLA 761530. The sponsor is Viridian Therapeutics INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat thyroid eye disease.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Lumvoa application. Whether Lumvoa had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Lumvoa approval facts

- Brand name: Lumvoa
- Active ingredient: veligrotug-vvze
- Sponsor: Viridian Therapeutics INC
- Application: BLA 761530
- FDA approval date: June 26, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
