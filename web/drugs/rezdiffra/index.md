# Rezdiffra (resmetirom) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rezdiffra (resmetirom) on March 14, 2024, under NDA 217785. The sponsor is Madrigal. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat noncirrhotic non-alcoholic steatohepatitis with moderate to advanced liver scarring.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Rezdiffra. FDA received the application on July 14, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.
