# Bysanti (milsaperidone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Bysanti (milsaperidone) on February 20, 2026, under NDA 220358. The sponsor is Vanda Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat schizophrenia and to treat manic or mixed episodes associated with bipolar I disorder.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Bysanti application. Whether Bysanti had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Bysanti approval facts

- Brand name: Bysanti
- Active ingredient: milsaperidone
- Sponsor: Vanda Pharms INC
- Application: NDA 220358
- FDA approval date: February 20, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Standard
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
