# Cosela (trilacicilib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Cosela (trilacicilib) on February 12, 2021, under NDA 214200. The sponsor is Pharmacosmos. FDA lists the approved use as: “To mitigate chemotherapy-induced myelosuppression in small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Cosela. FDA received the application on June 15, 2020 and approved it 242 days later.
