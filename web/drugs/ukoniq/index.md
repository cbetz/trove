# Ukoniq (umbralisib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ukoniq (umbralisib) on February 5, 2021, under NDA 213176. The sponsor is Tg Theraps. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat marginal zone lymphoma and follicular lymphoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (indication [A] only), orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation (indication [A] only) for Ukoniq. FDA received the application on June 15, 2020 and approved it 235 days later.

## Ukoniq approval facts

- Brand name: Ukoniq
- Active ingredient: umbralisib
- Sponsor: Tg Theraps
- Application: NDA 213176
- FDA approval date: February 5, 2021
- Approval year: 2021

- Receipt to approval: 235 days

- Review designation: Priority (indication [A] only)
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes (indication [A] only)
