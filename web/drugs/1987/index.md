# FDA novel drug approvals in 1987

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 22 novel drugs in 1987: 21 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 1 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

9 of the 22 (41%) had priority review and 4 had orphan drug designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 860 days (about 28.3 months).
