# Omvoh (mirikizumab-mrkz) FDA approval

The FDA approved Omvoh (mirikizumab-mrkz) on October 26, 2023, under BLA 761279. The sponsor is Eli Lilly And CO. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat ulcerative colitis.”

FDA reviewed Omvoh on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 30, 2022 and approved it 575 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Omvoh in the established pharmacologic class: Interleukin-23 Antagonist.
