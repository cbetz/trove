# Fayuvi (rebisufligene etisparvovec-hopf) FDA approval

The FDA approved Fayuvi (rebisufligene etisparvovec-hopf) on September 17, 2026. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125845; the manufacturer is Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Treatment of neurologic manifestations of mucopolysaccharidosis type IIIA (MPS IIIA, Sanfilippo syndrome type A) in pediatric patients with preserved neurodevelopmental function.”

## Fayuvi approval facts
