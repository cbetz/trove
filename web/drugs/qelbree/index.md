# Qelbree (viloxazine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Qelbree (viloxazine) on April 2, 2021, under NDA 211964. The sponsor is Supernus Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat attention deficit hyperactivity disorder.”

FDA reviewed Qelbree on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 8, 2019 and approved it 511 days later.

## Qelbree approval facts

- Brand name: Qelbree
- Active ingredient: viloxazine
- Sponsor: Supernus Pharms
- Application: NDA 211964
- FDA approval date: April 2, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: November 8, 2019
- Receipt to approval: 511 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
