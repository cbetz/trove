# Tevimbra (tislelizumab-jsgr) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tevimbra (tislelizumab-jsgr) on March 13, 2024, under BLA 761232. The sponsor is Beigene. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable or metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma.”

FDA reviewed Tevimbra on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 12, 2021 and approved it 975 days later.

## Tevimbra approval facts

- Brand name: Tevimbra
- Active ingredient: tislelizumab-jsgr
- Sponsor: Beigene
- Application: BLA 761232
- FDA approval date: March 13, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: July 12, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 975 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Intravenous
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
