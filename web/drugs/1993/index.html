FDA novel drug approvals in 1993

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 1993.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 27 novel drugs in 1993: 24 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 3 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

15 of the 27 (56%) had priority review, 8 had orphan drug designation and 1 were accelerated approvals. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 699 days (about 23.0 months).

What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
