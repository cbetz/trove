Tecelra (afamitresgene autoleucel) FDA approval

Adaptimmune LLC · BLA 125789 · approved Aug 1, 2024

The FDA approved Tecelra (afamitresgene autoleucel) on August 1, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125789; the manufacturer is Adaptimmune LLC.

What Tecelra is approved for

FDA's product page for Tecelra gives the indication as: Treatment of adults and pediatric patients 12 years of age and older with unresectable or metastatic synovial sarcoma who have received prior chemotherapy, are HLAA* 02:01P, -A*02:02P, -A*02:03P, or -A*02:06P positive and whose tumor expresses the MAGEA4 antigen as determined by FDA-approved or cleared companion diagnostic devices
