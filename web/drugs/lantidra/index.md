# Lantidra (donislecel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lantidra (donislecel) on June 28, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125734; the manufacturer is CellTrans Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “The treatment of adults with Type 1 diabetes who are unable to approach target HbA1c because of current repeated episodes of severe hypoglycemia despite intensive diabetes management and education.”

## Lantidra approval facts

- Brand name: Lantidra
- Active ingredient: donislecel
- Manufacturer: CellTrans Inc
- Application: BLA 125734
- FDA approval date: June 28, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)
