# Onswik (insulin efsitora alfa-gobe) FDA approval

The FDA approved Onswik (insulin efsitora alfa-gobe) on September 23, 2026, under BLA 761408. The sponsor is Eli Lilly And CO. FDA lists the approved use as: “To improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus, as an adjunct to diet and exercise.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Onswik application. Whether Onswik had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
