# Kimmtrak (tebentafusp-tebn) FDA approval

The FDA approved Kimmtrak (tebentafusp-tebn) on January 25, 2022, under BLA 761228. The sponsor is Immunocore LTD. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable or metastatic uveal melanoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Kimmtrak. FDA received the application on June 23, 2021 and approved it 216 days later.
