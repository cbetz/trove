# Tavneos (avacopan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tavneos (avacopan) on October 7, 2021, under NDA 214487. The sponsor is Chemocentryx. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat severe active anti-neutrophil cytoplasmic autoantibody-associated vasculitis (granulomatosis with polyangiitis and microscopic polyangiitis) in combination with standard therapy, including glucocorticoids.”

FDA reviewed Tavneos on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 7, 2020 and approved it 457 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Tavneos in the established pharmacologic class: Complement 5a Receptor Antagonist.
