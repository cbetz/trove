# Voxzogo (vosoritide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Voxzogo (vosoritide) on November 19, 2021, under NDA 214938. The sponsor is Biomarin Pharm. FDA lists the approved use as: “To improve growth in children five years of age and older with achondroplasia and open epiphyses.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Voxzogo. FDA received the application on August 20, 2020 and approved it 456 days later.
