# Krazati (adagrasib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Krazati (adagrasib) on December 12, 2022, under NDA 216340. The sponsor is Bristol. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer in adults who have received at least one prior systemic therapy.”

FDA reviewed Krazati on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 14, 2021 and approved it 363 days later.
