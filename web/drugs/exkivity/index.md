# Exkivity (mobocertinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Exkivity (mobocertinib) on September 15, 2021, under NDA 215310. The sponsor is Takeda Pharms USA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer with epidermal growth factor receptor exon 20 insertion mutations.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Exkivity. FDA received the application on February 26, 2021 and approved it 201 days later.
