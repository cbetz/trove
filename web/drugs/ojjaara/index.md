# Ojjaara (momelotinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ojjaara (momelotinib) on September 15, 2023, under NDA 216873. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat intermediate or high-risk myelofibrosis in adults with anemia.”

FDA reviewed Ojjaara on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 16, 2022 and approved it 456 days later.

## Ojjaara approval facts

- Brand name: Ojjaara

- Sponsor: Glaxosmithkline

- Receipt to approval: 456 days

- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
