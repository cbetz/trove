# Scemblix (asciminib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Scemblix (asciminib) on October 29, 2021, under NDA 215358. The sponsor is Novartis. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Philadelphia chromosome-positive chronic myeloid leukemia with disease that meets certain criteria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval (indication [A] only), breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Scemblix. FDA received the application on June 24, 2021 and approved it 127 days later.
