# Joenja (leniolisib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Joenja (leniolisib) on March 24, 2023, under NDA 217759. The sponsor is Pharming. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat activated phosphoinositide 3-kinase delta syndrome.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Joenja. FDA received the application on July 29, 2022 and approved it 238 days later.

## Joenja approval facts

- Brand name: Joenja
- Active ingredient: leniolisib
- Sponsor: Pharming
- Application: NDA 217759
- FDA approval date: March 24, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: July 29, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 238 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
