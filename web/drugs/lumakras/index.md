# Lumakras (sotorasib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lumakras (sotorasib) on May 28, 2021, under NDA 214665. The sponsor is Amgen INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat types of non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Lumakras. FDA received the application on December 16, 2020 and approved it 163 days later.

## Lumakras approval facts

- Brand name: Lumakras

- Dosage form: Tablet

- Orphan drug designation: Yes

- Fast track designation: Yes
