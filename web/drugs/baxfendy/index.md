# Baxfendy (baxdrostat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Baxfendy (baxdrostat) on May 15, 2026, under NDA 219878. The sponsor is Astrazeneca AB. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat hypertension in combination with other antihypertensive drugs.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Baxfendy application. Whether Baxfendy had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Baxfendy approval facts

- Brand name: Baxfendy
- Active ingredient: baxdrostat
- Sponsor: Astrazeneca AB
- Application: NDA 219878
- FDA approval date: May 15, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
