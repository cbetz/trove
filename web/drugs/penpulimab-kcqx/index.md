# penpulimab-kcqx (penpulimab-kcqx) FDA approval

The FDA approved penpulimab-kcqx (penpulimab-kcqx) on April 23, 2025, under BLA 761258. The sponsor is Akeso Biopharma Co., Ltd. FDA lists the approved use as: “In combination with either cisplatin or carboplatin and gemcitabine, to treat adults with recurrent or metastatic non-keratinizing nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC), or as a single agent while on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and at least one other prior line of therapy.”

FDA reviewed penpulimab-kcqx on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation (indication [B] only) and fast track designation (indication [B] only) at original approval. FDA received the application on July 16, 2021 and approved it 1,377 days later.
