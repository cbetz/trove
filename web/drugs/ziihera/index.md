# Ziihera (zanidatamab-hrii) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ziihera (zanidatamab-hrii) on November 20, 2024, under BLA 761416. The sponsor is Jazz Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+) biliary tract cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Ziihera. FDA received the application on March 29, 2024 and approved it 236 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Ziihera in the established pharmacologic class: Bispecific HER2-directed Antibody.
