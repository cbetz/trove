# Zegfrovy (sunvozertinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zegfrovy (sunvozertinib) on July 2, 2025, under NDA 219839. The sponsor is Dizal (Jiangsu) Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer with epidermal growth factor receptor exon 20 insertion mutations, as detected by an FDA-approved test, with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Zegfrovy. FDA received the application on November 7, 2024 and approved it 237 days later.
