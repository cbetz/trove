# Xenpozyme (Olipudase alfa) FDA approval

The FDA approved Xenpozyme (Olipudase alfa) on August 31, 2022, under BLA 761261. The sponsor is Genzyme CORP. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Acid Sphingomyelinase Deficiency.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Xenpozyme. FDA received the application on November 3, 2021 and approved it 301 days later.

## Xenpozyme approval facts

- Brand name: Xenpozyme
- Active ingredient: Olipudase alfa
- Sponsor: Genzyme CORP
- Application: BLA 761261
- FDA approval date: August 31, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA received application: November 3, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 301 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Injection
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
