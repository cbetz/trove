# Epkinly (epcoritamab-bysp) FDA approval

The FDA approved Epkinly (epcoritamab-bysp) on May 19, 2023, under BLA 761324. The sponsor is Genmab Us, INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (not otherwise specified) and high-grade B-cell lymphoma after two or more lines of systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and accelerated approval for Epkinly. FDA received the application on September 21, 2022 and approved it 240 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Epkinly in the established pharmacologic class: Bispecific CD20-directed CD3 T Cell Engager.
