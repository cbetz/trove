Jascayd (nerandomilast) FDA approval

Boehringer Ingelheim Pharmaceuticals, Inc. · NDA 218764 · approved Oct 7, 2025

The FDA approved Jascayd (nerandomilast) on October 7, 2025, under NDA 218764. The sponsor is Boehringer Ingelheim Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Jascayd. FDA received the application on February 7, 2025 and approved it 242 days later.
