# Trutakna (atacicept-vymj) FDA approval

The FDA approved Trutakna (atacicept-vymj) on July 7, 2026, under BLA 761486. The sponsor is Vera Therapeutics INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce proteinuria in adults with primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy at risk for disease progression.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Trutakna application. Whether Trutakna had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Trutakna approval facts

- Brand name: Trutakna
