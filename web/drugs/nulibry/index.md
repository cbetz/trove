# Nulibry (fosdenopterin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nulibry (fosdenopterin) on February 26, 2021, under NDA 214018. The sponsor is Sentynl Theraps INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce the risk of mortality in molybdenum cofactor deficiency Type A.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Nulibry. FDA received the application on June 29, 2020 and approved it 242 days later.

- Orphan drug designation: Yes

- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
