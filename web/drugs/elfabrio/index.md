# Elfabrio (pegunigalsidase alfa-iwxj) FDA approval

The FDA approved Elfabrio (pegunigalsidase alfa-iwxj) on May 9, 2023, under BLA 761161. The sponsor is Chiesi Farmaceutici SPA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat confirmed Fabry disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Elfabrio. FDA received the application on May 27, 2020 and approved it 1,077 days later.
