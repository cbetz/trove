# Iqirvo (elafibranor) FDA approval

The FDA approved Iqirvo (elafibranor) on June 10, 2024, under NDA 218860. The sponsor is Ipsen. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat primary biliary cholangitis in combination with ursodeoxycholic acid.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Iqirvo. FDA received the application on October 10, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Iqirvo in the established pharmacologic class: Peroxisome Proliferator-activated Receptor Agonist.
