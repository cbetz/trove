# Paxlovid (nirmatrelvir, ritonavir) FDA approval

The FDA approved Paxlovid (nirmatrelvir, ritonavir) on May 25, 2023, under NDA 217188. The sponsor is Pfizer. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat mild-to-moderate COVID-19 in adults at high risk for progression to severe COVID-19.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Paxlovid. FDA received the application on June 29, 2022 and approved it 330 days later.

## Paxlovid approval facts

- Brand name: Paxlovid
- Active ingredient: nirmatrelvir, ritonavir
- Sponsor: Pfizer
- Application: NDA 217188
- FDA approval date: May 25, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: June 29, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 330 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (material threat medical countermeasure)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
