# Romvimza (vimseltinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Romvimza (vimseltinib) on February 14, 2025, under NDA 219304. The sponsor is Deciphera Pharmaceuticals, LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumor for which surgical resection will potentially cause worsening functional limitation or severe morbidity.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Romvimza. FDA received the application on June 17, 2024 and approved it 242 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Romvimza in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
