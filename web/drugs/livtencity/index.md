# Livtencity (maribavir) FDA approval

The FDA approved Livtencity (maribavir) on November 23, 2021, under NDA 215596. The sponsor is Takeda Pharms USA. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat post-transplant cytomegalovirus (CMV) infection/disease that does not respond (with or without genetic mutations that cause resistance) to available antiviral treatment for CMV.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Livtencity. FDA received the application on March 23, 2021 and approved it 245 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Livtencity in the established pharmacologic class: Cytomegalovirus pUL97 Kinase Inhibitor.
