# Lenmeldy (atidarsagene autotemcel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lenmeldy (atidarsagene autotemcel) on March 18, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125758; the manufacturer is Orchard Therapeutics (Europe) Limited.

## Lenmeldy approval facts

- Brand name: Lenmeldy
- Active ingredient: atidarsagene autotemcel
- Manufacturer: Orchard Therapeutics (Europe) Limited
- Application: BLA 125758
- FDA approval date: March 18, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Lenmeldy is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: The treatment of children with pre-symptomatic late infantile (PSLI), pre-symptomatic early juvenile (PSEJ) or early symptomatic early juvenile (ESEJ) metachromatic leukodystrophy (MLD)
