# Pepaxto (melphalan flufenamide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Pepaxto (melphalan flufenamide) on February 26, 2021, under NDA 214383. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Pepaxto. FDA received the application on June 30, 2020 and approved it 241 days later.

## Pepaxto approval facts

- Brand name: Pepaxto
- Active ingredient: melphalan flufenamide
- Application: NDA 214383
- FDA approval date: February 26, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: June 30, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 241 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Injection
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
