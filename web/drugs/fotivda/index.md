# Fotivda (tivozanib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Fotivda (tivozanib) on March 10, 2021, under NDA 212904. The sponsor is Aveo Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat renal cell carcinoma.”

FDA reviewed Fotivda on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 31, 2020 and approved it 344 days later.

## Fotivda approval facts

- Brand name: Fotivda
- Active ingredient: tivozanib
- Sponsor: Aveo Pharms
- Application: NDA 212904
- FDA approval date: March 10, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: March 31, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 344 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
