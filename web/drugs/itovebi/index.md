# Itovebi (inavolisib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Itovebi (inavolisib) on October 10, 2024, under NDA 219249. The sponsor is Genentech INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Itovebi. FDA received the application on March 27, 2024 and approved it 197 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Itovebi in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
