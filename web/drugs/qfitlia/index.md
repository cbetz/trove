# Qfitlia (fitusiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Qfitlia (fitusiran) on March 28, 2025, under NDA 219019. The sponsor is Genzyme Corporation. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent or reduce the frequency of bleeding episodes in hemophilia A or B.”

FDA reviewed Qfitlia on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 28, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Qfitlia in the established pharmacologic classes: Antithrombin-directed RNA Interaction and Small Interfering RNA.
