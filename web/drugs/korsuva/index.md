# Korsuva (difelikefalin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Korsuva (difelikefalin) on August 23, 2021, under NDA 214916. The sponsor is Vifor Intl. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe pruritus associated with chronic kidney disease in certain populations.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Korsuva. FDA received the application on December 23, 2020 and approved it 243 days later.
