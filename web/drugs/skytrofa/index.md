# Skytrofa (lonapegsomatropin-tcgd) FDA approval

The FDA approved Skytrofa (lonapegsomatropin-tcgd) on August 25, 2021, under BLA 761177. The sponsor is Ascendis Pharma Encocrinology Div A/S. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat short stature due to inadequate secretion of endogenous growth hormone.”

FDA reviewed Skytrofa on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 25, 2020 and approved it 426 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Skytrofa in the established pharmacologic class: Recombinant Human Growth Hormone.
