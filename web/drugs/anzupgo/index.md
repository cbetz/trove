# Anzupgo (delgocitinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Anzupgo (delgocitinib) on July 23, 2025, under NDA 219155. The sponsor is LEO Pharma A/S. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe chronic hand eczema when topical corticosteroids are not advisable or produce an inadequate response.”

FDA reviewed Anzupgo on the standard review clock and recorded fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on July 23, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Anzupgo in the established pharmacologic class: Janus Kinase Inhibitor.
