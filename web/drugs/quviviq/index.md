# Quviviq (daridorexant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Quviviq (daridorexant) on January 7, 2022, under NDA 214985. The sponsor is Idorsia. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat insomnia.”

FDA reviewed Quviviq on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on January 8, 2021 and approved it 364 days later.

## Quviviq approval facts

- Brand name: Quviviq
- Active ingredient: daridorexant
- Sponsor: Idorsia
- Application: NDA 214985
- FDA approval date: January 7, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA received application: January 8, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 364 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
