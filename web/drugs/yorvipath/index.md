# Yorvipath (palopegteriparatide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Yorvipath (palopegteriparatide) on August 9, 2024, under NDA 216490. The sponsor is Ascendis Pharma Bone. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat hypoparathyroidism.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Yorvipath. FDA received the application on August 31, 2022 and approved it 709 days later.

## Yorvipath approval facts

- Brand name: Yorvipath
- Active ingredient: palopegteriparatide
- Sponsor: Ascendis Pharma Bone
- Application: NDA 216490
- FDA approval date: August 9, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: August 31, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 709 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Subcutaneous
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
