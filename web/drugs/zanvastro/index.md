# Zanvastro (zilganersen) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zanvastro (zilganersen) on September 3, 2026, under NDA 220210. The sponsor is Ionis Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Alexander disease in pediatric and adult patients.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Zanvastro application. Whether Zanvastro had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Zanvastro approval facts

- Brand name: Zanvastro
- Active ingredient: zilganersen
- Sponsor: Ionis Pharms INC
- Application: NDA 220210
- FDA approval date: September 3, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan status (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
