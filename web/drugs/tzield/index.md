# Tzield (teplizumab-mzwv) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tzield (teplizumab-mzwv) on November 17, 2022, under BLA 761183. The sponsor is Provention Bio INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To delay the onset of stage 3 type 1 diabetes.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Tzield. FDA received the application on November 2, 2020 and approved it 745 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Tzield in the established pharmacologic class: CD3-directed Antibody.
