# Elucirem (gadopiclenol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Elucirem (gadopiclenol) on September 21, 2022, under NDA 216986. The sponsor is Guerbet. FDA lists the approved use as: “To detect and visualize lesions, together with MRI, with abnormal vascularity in the central nervous system and the body.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review for Elucirem. FDA received the application on January 21, 2022 and approved it 243 days later.
