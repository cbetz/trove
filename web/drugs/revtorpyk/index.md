# Revtorpyk (gedatolisib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Revtorpyk (gedatolisib) on July 14, 2026, under NDA 219908. The sponsor is Celcuity. FDA lists the approved use as: “In combination with fulvestran, to treat hormone receptor-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative, locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a PIK3CA mutation detected following progression on or after treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Revtorpyk application. Whether Revtorpyk had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

FDA's labeling data puts Revtorpyk in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
