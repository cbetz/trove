# Emrelis (telisotuzumab vedotin-tllv) FDA approval

The FDA approved Emrelis (telisotuzumab vedotin-tllv) on May 14, 2025, under BLA 761384. The sponsor is AbbVie, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat locally advanced or metastatic, non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with high c-Met protein overexpression after prior systemic therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Emrelis. FDA received the application on September 27, 2024 and approved it 229 days later.
