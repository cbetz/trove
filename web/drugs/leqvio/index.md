# Leqvio (inclisiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Leqvio (inclisiran) on December 22, 2021, under NDA 214012. The sponsor is Novartis. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia or clinical atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease as an add-on therapy.”

FDA reviewed Leqvio on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 23, 2019 and approved it 730 days later.
