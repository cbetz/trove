# Ekterly (sebetralstat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ekterly (sebetralstat) on July 3, 2025, under NDA 219301. The sponsor is KalVista Pharmaceuticals Limited. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat acute attacks of hereditary angioedema.”

FDA reviewed Ekterly on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 17, 2024 and approved it 381 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Ekterly in the established pharmacologic class: Plasma Kallikrein Inhibitor.
