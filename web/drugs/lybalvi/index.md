# Lybalvi (olanzapine and samidorphan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lybalvi (olanzapine and samidorphan) on May 28, 2021, under NDA 213378. The sponsor is Alkermes INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat schizophrenia and certain aspects of bipolar I disorder.”

FDA reviewed Lybalvi on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 15, 2019 and approved it 560 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Lybalvi in the established pharmacologic class: Atypical Antipsychotic.
