# Izervay (avacincaptad pegol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Izervay (avacincaptad pegol) on August 4, 2023, under NDA 217225. The sponsor is Astellas. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat geographic atrophy secondary to age-related macular degeneration.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Izervay. FDA received the application on December 19, 2022 and approved it 228 days later.
