# Welgemda (ribitol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Welgemda (ribitol) on November 23, 2026, under NDA 220905. The sponsor is Ml Bio Solutions INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat limb-girdle muscular dystrophy type 2I/R9.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Welgemda application. Whether Welgemda had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Welgemda approval facts

- Brand name: Welgemda
- Active ingredient: ribitol
- Sponsor: Ml Bio Solutions INC
- Application: NDA 220905
- FDA approval date: November 23, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan status (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
