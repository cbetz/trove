# Flyrcado (flurpiridaz F 18) FDA approval

The FDA approved Flyrcado (flurpiridaz F 18) on September 27, 2024, under NDA 215168. The sponsor is Ge Hlthcare. FDA lists the approved use as: “A radioactive diagnostic drug to evaluate for myocardial ischemia and infarction.”

FDA reviewed Flyrcado on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 27, 2023 and approved it 366 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Flyrcado in the established pharmacologic class: Radioactive Diagnostic Agent.
