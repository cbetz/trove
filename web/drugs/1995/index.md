# FDA novel drug approvals in 1995

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 30 novel drugs in 1995: 29 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 1 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

11 of the 30 (37%) had priority review, 5 had orphan drug designation and 4 were accelerated approvals. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 530 days (about 17.4 months).
