# Itvisma (onasemnogene abeparvovec-brve) FDA approval

The FDA approved Itvisma (onasemnogene abeparvovec-brve) on November 24, 2025. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125856; the manufacturer is Novartis Gene Therapies, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For the treatment of spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) in adult and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with confirmed mutation in survival motor neuron 1 (SMN1) gene.”
