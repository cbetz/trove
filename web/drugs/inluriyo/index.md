# Inluriyo (imlunestrant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Inluriyo (imlunestrant) on September 25, 2025, under NDA 218881. The sponsor is Eli Lilly and Company. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat estrogen receptor-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative, estrogen receptor-1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer with disease progression following at least one line of endocrine therapy.”

FDA reviewed Inluriyo on the standard review clock and recorded fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 31, 2024 and approved it 329 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Inluriyo in the established pharmacologic class: Estrogen Receptor Antagonist.
