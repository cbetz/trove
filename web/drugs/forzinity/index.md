# Forzinity (elamipretide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Forzinity (elamipretide) on September 19, 2025, under NDA 215244. The sponsor is Stealth BioTherapeutics, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To improve muscle strength in patients with Barth syndrome weighing at least 30 kg.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Forzinity. FDA received the application on January 29, 2024 and approved it 599 days later.
