# Sofdra (sofpironium) FDA approval

The FDA approved Sofdra (sofpironium) on June 18, 2024, under NDA 217347. The sponsor is Botanix Sb. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat primary axillary hyperhidrosis.”

FDA reviewed Sofdra on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 23, 2022 and approved it 634 days later.

## Sofdra approval facts

- Brand name: Sofdra

- Sponsor: Botanix Sb
- Application: NDA 217347
- FDA approval date: June 18, 2024
- Approval year: 2024

- Receipt to approval: 634 days

- Review designation: Standard

- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
