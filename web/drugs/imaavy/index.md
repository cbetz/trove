# Imaavy (nipocalimab-aahu) FDA approval

The FDA approved Imaavy (nipocalimab-aahu) on April 29, 2025, under BLA 761430. The sponsor is Janssen Biotech, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat generalized myasthenia gravis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Imaavy. FDA received the application on August 29, 2024 and approved it 243 days later.
