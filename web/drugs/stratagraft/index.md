# Stratagraft (allogeneic cultured keratinocytes and dermal fibroblasts in murine collagen-dsat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Stratagraft (allogeneic cultured keratinocytes and dermal fibroblasts in murine collagen-dsat) on June 15, 2021. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125730; the manufacturer is Stratatech Corporation. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “To promote durable wound closure & regenerative healing in the treatment of adult patients with debrided thermal burns that contain intact dermal elements, and for which surgical intervention is clinically indicated.”

## Stratagraft approval facts

- Brand name: Stratagraft

- Manufacturer: Stratatech Corporation
