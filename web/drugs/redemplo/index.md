# Redemplo (plozasiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Redemplo (plozasiran) on November 18, 2025, under NDA 219947. The sponsor is Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce triglycerides in adults with familial chylomicronemia syndrome.”

FDA reviewed Redemplo on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 18, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Redemplo in the established pharmacologic classes: APOC-III-directed RNA Interaction and Small Interfering RNA.
