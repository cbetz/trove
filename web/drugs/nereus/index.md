# Nereus (tradipitant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nereus (tradipitant) on December 30, 2025, under NDA 220152. The sponsor is Vanda Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat vomiting associated with motion.”

FDA reviewed Nereus on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 30, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.
