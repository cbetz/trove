# Miplyffa (arimoclomol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Miplyffa (arimoclomol) on September 20, 2024, under NDA 214927. The sponsor is Zevra Denmark. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Niemann-Pick disease type C.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Miplyffa. FDA received the application on July 17, 2020 and approved it 1,526 days later.
