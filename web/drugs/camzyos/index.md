# Camzyos (mavacamten) FDA approval

The FDA approved Camzyos (mavacamten) on April 28, 2022, under NDA 214998. The sponsor is Bristol. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat certain classes of obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.”

FDA reviewed Camzyos on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation at original approval. FDA received the application on January 28, 2021 and approved it 455 days later.

## Camzyos approval facts

- Brand name: Camzyos
- Active ingredient: mavacamten
- Sponsor: Bristol
- Application: NDA 214998
- FDA approval date: April 28, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA received application: January 28, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 455 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
