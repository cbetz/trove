# NexoBrid (anacaulase-bcdb) FDA approval

The FDA approved NexoBrid (anacaulase-bcdb) on December 28, 2022, under BLA 761192. The sponsor is Mediwound, LTD. FDA lists the approved use as: “To remove eschar in adults with deep partial thickness or full thickness thermal burns.”

FDA reviewed NexoBrid on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on June 29, 2020 and approved it 912 days later.
