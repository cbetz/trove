# Velsipity (etrasimod) FDA approval

The FDA approved Velsipity (etrasimod) on October 12, 2023, under NDA 216956. The sponsor is Pfizer. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis in adults.”

FDA reviewed Velsipity on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 14, 2022 and approved it 363 days later.

## Velsipity approval facts

- Brand name: Velsipity
- Active ingredient: etrasimod
- Sponsor: Pfizer
- Application: NDA 216956
- FDA approval date: October 12, 2023
- Approval year: 2023

- Receipt to approval: 363 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
