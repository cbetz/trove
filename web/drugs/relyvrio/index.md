# Relyvrio (sodium phenylbutyrate/taurursodiol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Relyvrio (sodium phenylbutyrate/taurursodiol) on September 29, 2022, under NDA 216660. The sponsor is Amylyx. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS).”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Relyvrio. FDA received the application on October 29, 2021 and approved it 335 days later.

## Relyvrio approval facts

- Brand name: Relyvrio
- Active ingredient: sodium phenylbutyrate/taurursodiol
- Sponsor: Amylyx
- Application: NDA 216660
- FDA approval date: September 29, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA received application: October 29, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 335 days
- Dosage form: For Suspension
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
