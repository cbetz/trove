# Casgevy (exagamglogene autotemcel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Casgevy (exagamglogene autotemcel) on December 8, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125787; the manufacturer is Vertex Pharmaceuticals Incorporated.

## Casgevy approval facts

- Brand name: Casgevy
- Active ingredient: exagamglogene autotemcel
- Manufacturer: Vertex Pharmaceuticals Incorporated
- Application: BLA 125787
- FDA approval date: December 8, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Casgevy is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: STN 125787 - For the treatment of patients aged 2 years and older with: Sickle cell disease (SCD) with recurrent vaso-occlusive crises (VOCs). Transfusion-dependent β-thalassemia (TDT); STN 125785 - Indicated for the treatment of patients aged 12 years and older with sickle cell disease (SCD) with recurrent vaso occlusive crises (VOCs) and transfusion-dependent ß-thalassemia (TDT)
