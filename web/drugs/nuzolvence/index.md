# Nuzolvence (zoliflodacin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nuzolvence (zoliflodacin) on December 12, 2025, under NDA 219491. The sponsor is Entasis Therapeutics Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat uncomplicated urogenital gonorrhea due to Neisseria gonorrhoeae.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Nuzolvence. FDA received the application on April 15, 2025 and approved it 241 days later.
