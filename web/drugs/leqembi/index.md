# Leqembi (lecanemab-irmb) FDA approval

The FDA approved Leqembi (lecanemab-irmb) on January 6, 2023, under BLA 761269. The sponsor is Eisai INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Alzheimer’s disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Leqembi. FDA received the application on May 6, 2022 and approved it 245 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Leqembi in the established pharmacologic class: Amyloid Beta-directed Antibody.
