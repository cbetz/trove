# Foundayo (orforglipron) FDA approval

The FDA approved Foundayo (orforglipron) on April 1, 2026, under NDA 220934. The sponsor is Eli Lilly And CO. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce excess body weight and maintain weight reduction long term in adults with obesity or adults with overweight in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbid condition, in combination with a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Foundayo application. Whether Foundayo had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
