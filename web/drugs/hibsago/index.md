# Hibsago (bepirovirsen) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hibsago (bepirovirsen) on October 20, 2026, under NDA 221276. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat chronic hepatitis B virus infection in certain adults without cirrhosis, in combination with existing antiviral therapy.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Hibsago application. Whether Hibsago had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Hibsago approval facts

- Brand name: Hibsago
