Icotyde (icotrokinra) FDA approval

Janssen Biotech · NDA 220149 · approved Mar 17, 2026

The FDA approved Icotyde (icotrokinra) on March 17, 2026, under NDA 220149. The sponsor is Janssen Biotech. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in patients 12 years and older who weigh at least 40 kg and who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Icotyde application. Whether Icotyde had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
