# Zelsuvmi (berdazimer) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zelsuvmi (berdazimer) on January 5, 2024, under NDA 217424. The sponsor is Lnhc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat molluscum contagiosum.”

FDA reviewed Zelsuvmi on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on January 5, 2023 and approved it 365 days later.

## Zelsuvmi approval facts

- Brand name: Zelsuvmi
- Active ingredient: berdazimer
- Sponsor: Lnhc
- Application: NDA 217424
- FDA approval date: January 5, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: January 5, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 365 days
- Dosage form: GEL
- Route of administration: Topical
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
