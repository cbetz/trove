# Rethymic (allogeneic processed thymus tissue – agdc) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rethymic (allogeneic processed thymus tissue – agdc) on October 8, 2021. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125685; the manufacturer is Enzyvant Therapeutics GmbH. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For immune reconstitution in pediatric patients with congenital athymia.”

## Rethymic approval facts

- Brand name: Rethymic
- Active ingredient: allogeneic processed thymus tissue – agdc
- Manufacturer: Enzyvant Therapeutics GmbH
- Application: BLA 125685
- FDA approval date: October 8, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)
