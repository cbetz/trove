# Adquey (difamilast) FDA approval

The FDA approved Adquey (difamilast) on February 12, 2026, under NDA 219474. The sponsor is Acrotech Biopharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat mild to moderate atopic dermatitis.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Adquey application. Whether Adquey had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

FDA's labeling data puts Adquey in the established pharmacologic class: Phosphodiesterase 4 Inhibitor.

## Adquey approval facts

- Brand name: Adquey
