# Lembaz (adrabetadex) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lembaz (adrabetadex) on November 17, 2026, under NDA 220996. The sponsor is Mandos LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat a severe form of Niemann-Pick disease type C.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Lembaz application. Whether Lembaz had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Lembaz approval facts

- Brand name: Lembaz
- Active ingredient: adrabetadex
- Sponsor: Mandos LLC
- Application: NDA 220996
- FDA approval date: November 17, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan status (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
