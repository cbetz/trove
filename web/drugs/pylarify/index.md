# Pylarify (piflufolastat F 18) FDA approval

The FDA approved Pylarify (piflufolastat F 18) on May 26, 2021, under NDA 214793. The sponsor is Progenics Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To identify prostate-specific membrane antigen-positive lesions in prostate cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review for Pylarify. FDA received the application on September 29, 2020 and approved it 239 days later.
