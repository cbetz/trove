# FDA novel drug approvals in 2014

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 41 novel drugs in 2014: 30 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 11 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

25 of the 41 (61%) had priority review, 17 had orphan drug designation, 8 were accelerated approvals, 9 had breakthrough therapy designation and 17 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 334 days (about 11.0 months).
