# Kygevvi (doxecitine and doxribtimine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Kygevvi (doxecitine and doxribtimine) on November 3, 2025, under NDA 219792. The sponsor is UCB, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat thymidine kinase 2 deficiency in patients who start to show symptoms when they are 12 years old or younger.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Kygevvi. FDA received the application on December 16, 2024 and approved it 322 days later.
