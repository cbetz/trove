# Crenessity (crinecerfont) FDA approval

The FDA approved Crenessity (crinecerfont) on December 13, 2024, under NDA 218808. The sponsor is Neurocrine. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Crenessity. FDA received the application on April 29, 2024 and approved it 228 days later.

## Crenessity approval facts

- Brand name: Crenessity
