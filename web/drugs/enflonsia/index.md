# Enflonsia (clesrovimab-cfor) FDA approval

The FDA approved Enflonsia (clesrovimab-cfor) on June 9, 2025, under BLA 761432. The sponsor is Merck Sharp and Dohme, LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) lower respiratory tract disease in neonates and infants who are born during or entering their first RSV season.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) and fast track designation for Enflonsia. FDA received the application on October 10, 2024 and approved it 242 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Enflonsia in the established pharmacologic class: Respiratory Syncytial Virus Anti-F Protein Monoclonal Antibody.
