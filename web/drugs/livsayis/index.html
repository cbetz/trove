Livsayis (cemdisiran) FDA approval

Regeneron Pharmaceuticals · NDA 221251 · approved Nov 18, 2026

The FDA approved Livsayis (cemdisiran) on November 18, 2026, under NDA 221251. The sponsor is Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat anti-acetylcholine receptor antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Livsayis application. Whether Livsayis had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
