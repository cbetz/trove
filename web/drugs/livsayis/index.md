# Livsayis (cemdisiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Livsayis (cemdisiran) on November 18, 2026, under NDA 221251. The sponsor is Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat anti-acetylcholine receptor antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Livsayis application. Whether Livsayis had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Livsayis approval facts

- Brand name: Livsayis
- Active ingredient: cemdisiran
- Sponsor: Regeneron Pharmaceuticals
- Application: NDA 221251
- FDA approval date: November 18, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan status (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
