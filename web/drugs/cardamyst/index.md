# Cardamyst (etripamil) FDA approval

The FDA approved Cardamyst (etripamil) on December 12, 2025, under NDA 218571. The sponsor is Milestone Pharmaceuticals USA, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat episodes of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia.”

FDA reviewed Cardamyst on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 27, 2024 and approved it 625 days later.
