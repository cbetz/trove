# Skyclarys (omaveloxolone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Skyclarys (omaveloxolone) on February 28, 2023, under NDA 216718. The sponsor is Biogen Us. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Friedrich’s ataxia.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Skyclarys. FDA received the application on March 30, 2022 and approved it 335 days later.

## Skyclarys approval facts

- Brand name: Skyclarys

- Sponsor: Biogen Us
- Application: NDA 216718
- FDA approval date: February 28, 2023

- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)

- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
