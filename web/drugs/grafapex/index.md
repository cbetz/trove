# Grafapex (treosulfan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Grafapex (treosulfan) on January 21, 2025, under NDA 214759. The sponsor is medac Gesellschaft fur klinische Spezialpraparate mbH. FDA lists the approved use as: “For use in combination with fludarabine as a preparative regimen for allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation for acute myeloid leukemia and myelodysplastic syndrome.”

FDA reviewed Grafapex on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on August 11, 2020 and approved it 1,624 days later.
