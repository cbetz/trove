# Cabenuva (cabotegravir and rilpivirine (co-packaged)) FDA approval

The FDA approved Cabenuva (cabotegravir and rilpivirine (co-packaged)) on January 21, 2021, under NDA 212888. The sponsor is Viiv Hlthcare. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat HIV.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Cabenuva. FDA received the application on April 29, 2019 and approved it 633 days later.

## Cabenuva approval facts

- Brand name: Cabenuva
- Active ingredient: cabotegravir and rilpivirine (co-packaged)
- Sponsor: Viiv Hlthcare
- Application: NDA 212888
- FDA approval date: January 21, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: April 29, 2019
- Receipt to approval: 633 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Injection
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity and Type 4 - New Combination
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
