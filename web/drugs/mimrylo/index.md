# Mimrylo (rusfertide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Mimrylo (rusfertide) on August 28, 2026, under NDA 220605. The sponsor is Takeda Pharms Am. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat erythrocytosis in adults with polycythemia vera.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Mimrylo application. Whether Mimrylo had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Mimrylo approval facts

- Brand name: Mimrylo
- Active ingredient: rusfertide
- Sponsor: Takeda Pharms Am
- Application: NDA 220605
- FDA approval date: August 28, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Orphan status (Drugs@FDA): Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
