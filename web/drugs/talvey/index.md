# Talvey (talquetamab-tgvs) FDA approval

The FDA approved Talvey (talquetamab-tgvs) on August 9, 2023, under BLA 761342. The sponsor is Janssen Biotech. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least four prior therapies.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Talvey. FDA received the application on December 9, 2022 and approved it 243 days later.
