# Bizengri (zenocutuzumab-zbco) FDA approval

The FDA approved Bizengri (zenocutuzumab-zbco) on December 4, 2024, under BLA 761352. The sponsor is Merus N.v. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-small cell lung cancer and pancreatic adenocarcinoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation (indication [B] only), accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Bizengri. FDA received the application on March 4, 2024 and approved it 275 days later.
