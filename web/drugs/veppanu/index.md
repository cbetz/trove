# Veppanu (vepdegestrant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Veppanu (vepdegestrant) on May 1, 2026, under NDA 219835. The sponsor is Rigel Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat estrogen receptor-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer with disease progression following at least one line of endocrine therapy.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Veppanu application. Whether Veppanu had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
