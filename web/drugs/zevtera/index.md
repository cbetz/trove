# Zevtera (ceftobiprole medocaril sodium) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zevtera (ceftobiprole medocaril sodium) on April 3, 2024, under NDA 218275. The sponsor is Istx. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat certain bloodstream infections, bacterial skin and associated tissue infections, and community-acquired bacterial pneumonia.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation (indication [A] only) and qualified infectious disease product designation for Zevtera. FDA received the application on August 3, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.
