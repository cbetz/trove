# FDA novel drug approvals in 2001

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 29 novel drugs in 2001: 24 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 5 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

9 of the 29 (31%) had priority review, 4 had orphan drug designation, 3 were accelerated approvals and 4 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 501 days (about 16.5 months).
