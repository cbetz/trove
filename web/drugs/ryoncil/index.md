# Ryoncil (remestemcel-L-rknd) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ryoncil (remestemcel-L-rknd) on December 18, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125706; the manufacturer is Mesoblast, Inc.

## Ryoncil approval facts

- Brand name: Ryoncil
- Active ingredient: remestemcel-L-rknd
- Manufacturer: Mesoblast, Inc
- Application: BLA 125706
- FDA approval date: December 18, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Ryoncil is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: The treatment of steroid-refractory acute graft versus host disease (SR-aGvHD) in pediatric patients 2 months of age and older
