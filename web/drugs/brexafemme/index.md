# Brexafemme (ibrexafungerp) FDA approval

The FDA approved Brexafemme (ibrexafungerp) on June 1, 2021, under NDA 214900. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat vulvovaginal candidiasis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Brexafemme. FDA received the application on October 1, 2020 and approved it 243 days later.
