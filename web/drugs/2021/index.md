# FDA novel drug approvals in 2021

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 50 novel drugs in 2021: 36 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 14 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

35 of the 50 (70%) had priority review, 26 had orphan drug designation, 14 were accelerated approvals, 14 had breakthrough therapy designation and 18 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 245 days (about 8.0 months).

FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) separately approved 4 cell and gene therapy products in 2021, listed in the second table.
