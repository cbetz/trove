# Lyrfigtu (lirafugratinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lyrfigtu (lirafugratinib) on September 23, 2026, under NDA 220425. The sponsor is Elevar. FDA lists the approved use as: “To adults with previously treated unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring a fibroblast growth factor receptor 2 gene fusion or other rearrangement.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Lyrfigtu application. Whether Lyrfigtu had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
