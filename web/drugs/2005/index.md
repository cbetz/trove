# FDA novel drug approvals in 2005

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 20 novel drugs in 2005: 13 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 7 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

15 of the 20 (75%) had priority review, 7 had orphan drug designation, 3 were accelerated approvals and 6 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 259 days (about 8.5 months).
