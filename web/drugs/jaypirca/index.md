# Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib) on January 27, 2023, under NDA 216059. The sponsor is Loxo Oncol. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma in adults who have had at least two lines of systemic therapy, including a BTK inhibitor.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Jaypirca. FDA received the application on May 27, 2022 and approved it 245 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Jaypirca in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
