# Opdualag (nivolumab and relatlimab-rmbw) FDA approval

The FDA approved Opdualag (nivolumab and relatlimab-rmbw) on March 18, 2022, under BLA 761234. The sponsor is Bristol Myers Squibb. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat unresectable or metastatic melanoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Opdualag. FDA received the application on July 19, 2021 and approved it 242 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Opdualag in the established pharmacologic classes: Programmed Death Receptor-1 Blocking Antibody and Lymphocyte Activation Gene-3 Blocker.
