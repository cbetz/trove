# Lipfendra (enlicitide decanoate) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lipfendra (enlicitide decanoate) on July 15, 2026, under NDA 220848. The sponsor is Msd. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Lipfendra application. Whether Lipfendra had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Lipfendra approval facts

- Brand name: Lipfendra
- Active ingredient: enlicitide decanoate
- Sponsor: Msd
- Application: NDA 220848
- FDA approval date: July 15, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
