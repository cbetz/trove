# FDA novel drug approvals in 2007

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 18 novel drugs in 2007: 16 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 2 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

8 of the 18 (44%) had priority review, 6 had orphan drug designation, 3 were accelerated approvals and 7 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 302 days (about 9.9 months).
