# Rylaze (asparaginase erwinia chrysanthemi (recombinant)-rywn) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rylaze (asparaginase erwinia chrysanthemi (recombinant)-rywn) on June 30, 2021, under BLA 761179. The sponsor is Jazz Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat acute lymphoblastic leukemia and lymphoblastic lymphoma in patients who are allergic to E. coli-derived asparaginase products, as a component of a chemotherapy regimen.”

FDA reviewed Rylaze on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on April 30, 2021 and approved it 61 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Rylaze in the established pharmacologic class: Asparagine-specific Enzyme.
