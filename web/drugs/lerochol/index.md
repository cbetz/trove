# Lerochol (lerodalcibep-liga) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lerochol (lerodalcibep-liga) on December 12, 2025, under BLA 761427. The sponsor is LIB Therapeutics, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol in adults with hypercholesterolemia, including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia, as an adjunct to diet and exercise.”

FDA reviewed Lerochol on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on December 12, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.
