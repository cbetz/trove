# Skysona (elivaldogene autotemcel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Skysona (elivaldogene autotemcel) on September 16, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125755; the manufacturer is bluebird bio, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “SKYSONA is indicated to slow the progression of neurologic dysfunction in boys 4- 17 years of age with early, active cerebral adrenoleukodystrophy (CALD) without an available human leukocyte antigen (HLA)-matched donor for allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant.”
