# Vonjo (pacritinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vonjo (pacritinib) on February 28, 2022, under NDA 208712. The sponsor is Sobi. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat intermediate or high-risk primary or secondary myelofibrosis in adults with low platelets.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Vonjo. FDA received the application on March 30, 2021 and approved it 335 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Vonjo in the established pharmacologic class: Kinase Inhibitor.
