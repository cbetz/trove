# Verquvo (vericiguat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Verquvo (vericiguat) on January 19, 2021, under NDA 214377. The sponsor is Msd. FDA lists the approved use as: “To mitigate the risk of cardiovascular death and hospitalization for chronic heart failure.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and fast track designation for Verquvo. FDA received the application on May 20, 2020 and approved it 244 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Verquvo in the established pharmacologic class: Soluble Guanylate Cyclase Stimulator.
