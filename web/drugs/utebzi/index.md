# Utebzi (tebipenem pivoxil) FDA approval

The FDA approved Utebzi (tebipenem pivoxil) on June 17, 2026, under NDA 215960. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat complicated urinary tract infections, including pyelonephritis, caused by several susceptible microorganisms in adults who have limited or no alternative oral treatment options.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Utebzi application. Whether Utebzi had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
