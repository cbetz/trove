# Lunsumio (mosunetuzumab-axgb) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lunsumio (mosunetuzumab-axgb) on December 22, 2022, under BLA 761263. The sponsor is Genentech INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma, a type of non-Hodgkin lymphoma.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Lunsumio. FDA received the application on April 29, 2022 and approved it 237 days later.
