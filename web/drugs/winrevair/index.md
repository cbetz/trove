# Winrevair (sotatercept-csrk) FDA approval

The FDA approved Winrevair (sotatercept-csrk) on March 26, 2024, under BLA 761363. The sponsor is Merck Sharp Dohme. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat pulmonary arterial hypertension.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Winrevair. FDA received the application on July 26, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.

## Winrevair approval facts
