# Carvykti (ciltacabtagene autoleucel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Carvykti (ciltacabtagene autoleucel) on February 28, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125746; the manufacturer is Janssen Biotech, Inc.

## Carvykti approval facts

- Brand name: Carvykti
- Active ingredient: ciltacabtagene autoleucel
- Manufacturer: Janssen Biotech, Inc
- Application: BLA 125746
- FDA approval date: February 28, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Carvykti is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least 1 prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent, and are refractory to lenalidomide
