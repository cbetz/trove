Carvykti (ciltacabtagene autoleucel) FDA approval

Janssen Biotech, Inc · BLA 125746 · approved Feb 28, 2022

The FDA approved Carvykti (ciltacabtagene autoleucel) on February 28, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125746; the manufacturer is Janssen Biotech, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least 1 prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent, and are refractory to lenalidomide.”
