# Daybue (trofinetide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Daybue (trofinetide) on March 10, 2023, under NDA 217026. The sponsor is Acadia Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Rett syndrome.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Daybue. FDA received the application on July 12, 2022 and approved it 241 days later.

## Daybue approval facts

- Brand name: Daybue
- Active ingredient: trofinetide
- Sponsor: Acadia Pharms INC
- Application: NDA 217026
- FDA approval date: March 10, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: July 12, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 241 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
