# Omlonti (oomidenepag isopropyl ophthalmic solution) FDA approval

The FDA approved Omlonti (oomidenepag isopropyl ophthalmic solution) on September 22, 2022, under NDA 215092. The sponsor is Ocuvex Therap. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce elevated intraocular pressure in patients with open‑angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension.”

FDA reviewed Omlonti on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on November 19, 2020 and approved it 672 days later.
