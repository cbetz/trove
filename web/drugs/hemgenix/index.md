# Hemgenix (etranacogene dezaparvovec-drlb) FDA approval

The FDA approved Hemgenix (etranacogene dezaparvovec-drlb) on November 22, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125772; the manufacturer is CSL Behring LLC. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For treatment of adults with Hemophilia B (congenital Factor IX deficiency) who: Currently use Factor IX prophylaxis therapy, or have current or historical life-threatening hemorrhage, or have repeated, serious spontaneous bleeding episodes.”
