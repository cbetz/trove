# Ztalmy (ganaxolone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ztalmy (ganaxolone) on March 18, 2022, under NDA 215904. The sponsor is Immedica Pharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat seizures in cyclin-dependent kinase-like 5 deficiency disorder.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Ztalmy. FDA received the application on July 20, 2021 and approved it 241 days later.

## Ztalmy approval facts

- Brand name: Ztalmy
- Active ingredient: ganaxolone
- Sponsor: Immedica Pharma
- Application: NDA 215904
- FDA approval date: March 18, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA received application: July 20, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 241 days
- Dosage form: Suspension
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
