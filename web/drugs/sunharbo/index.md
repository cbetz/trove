# Sunharbo (asundexian) FDA approval

The FDA approved Sunharbo (asundexian) on November 9, 2026, under NDA 221291. The sponsor is Bayer Healthcare. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce the risk of stroke in adults following a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or a high-risk transient ischemic attack.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Sunharbo application. Whether Sunharbo had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.

## Sunharbo approval facts

- Brand name: Sunharbo
- Active ingredient: asundexian
- Sponsor: Bayer Healthcare
- Application: NDA 221291
- FDA approval date: November 9, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- Review priority (Drugs@FDA): Priority
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
