# Papzimeos (zopapogene imadenovec-drba) FDA approval

The FDA approved Papzimeos (zopapogene imadenovec-drba) on August 14, 2025. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125832; the manufacturer is Precigen, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For the treatment of adults with recurrent respiratory papillomatosis.”

## Papzimeos approval facts

- Brand name: Papzimeos
- Active ingredient: zopapogene imadenovec-drba
- Manufacturer: Precigen, Inc
- Application: BLA 125832
- FDA approval date: August 14, 2025
- Approval year: 2025
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)
