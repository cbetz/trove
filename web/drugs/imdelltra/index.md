# Imdelltra (tarlatamab-dlle) FDA approval

The FDA approved Imdelltra (tarlatamab-dlle) on May 16, 2024, under BLA 761344. The sponsor is Amgen INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat extensive stage small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Imdelltra. FDA received the application on October 12, 2023 and approved it 217 days later.
