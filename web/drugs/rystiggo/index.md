# Rystiggo (rozanolixizumab-noli) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rystiggo (rozanolixizumab-noli) on June 26, 2023, under BLA 761286. The sponsor is Ucb INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat generalized myasthenia gravis in adults who are anti-acetylcholine receptor- or anti-muscle-specific tyrosine kinase antibody-positive.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Rystiggo. FDA received the application on October 24, 2022 and approved it 245 days later.
