# Filsuvez (birch triterpenes) FDA approval

The FDA approved Filsuvez (birch triterpenes) on December 18, 2023, under NDA 215064. The sponsor is Chiesi. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat wounds associated with dystrophic and junctional epidermolysis bullosa.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Filsuvez. FDA received the application on March 30, 2021 and approved it 993 days later.
