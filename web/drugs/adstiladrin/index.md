# Adstiladrin (nadofaragene firadenovec-vcng) FDA approval

The FDA approved Adstiladrin (nadofaragene firadenovec-vcng) on December 16, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125700; the manufacturer is Ferring Pharmaceuticals A/S.

## Adstiladrin approval facts

- Brand name: Adstiladrin
- Active ingredient: nadofaragene firadenovec-vcng
- Manufacturer: Ferring Pharmaceuticals A/S
- Application: BLA 125700
- FDA approval date: December 16, 2022
- Approval year: 2022
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Adstiladrin is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: High-grade, Bacillus Calmette-Guerin (BCG) unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer
