# FDA novel drug approvals in 1988

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 20 novel drugs in 1988: 20 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 0 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

10 of the 20 (50%) had priority review and 4 had orphan drug designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 1,036 days (about 34.0 months).
