# Vyjuvek (beremagene geperpavec-svdt) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vyjuvek (beremagene geperpavec-svdt) on May 19, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125774; the manufacturer is Krystal Biotech, Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Treatment of wounds in adult and pediatric patients with dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa with mutation(s) in the collagen type VII alpha 1 chain (COL7A1) gene.”
