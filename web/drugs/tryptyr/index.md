# Tryptyr (acoltremon) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tryptyr (acoltremon) on May 28, 2025, under NDA 217370. The sponsor is Alcon Laboratories, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat the signs and symptoms of dry eye disease.”

FDA reviewed Tryptyr on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on May 30, 2024 and approved it 363 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Tryptyr in the established pharmacologic class: TRPM8 Thermoreceptor Agonist.
