# Aduhelm (aducanumab-avwa) FDA approval

The FDA approved Aduhelm (aducanumab-avwa) on June 7, 2021, under BLA 761178. The sponsor is Biogen INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Alzheimer’s disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and fast track designation for Aduhelm. FDA received the application on July 7, 2020 and approved it 335 days later.

## Aduhelm approval facts

- Brand name: Aduhelm
- Active ingredient: aducanumab-avwa
- Sponsor: Biogen INC
- Application: BLA 761178
- FDA approval date: June 7, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: July 7, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 335 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Injection
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: Yes
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
