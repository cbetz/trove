# Agamree (vamorolone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Agamree (vamorolone) on October 26, 2023, under NDA 215239. The sponsor is Catalyst Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Duchenne muscular dystrophy.”

FDA reviewed Agamree on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 26, 2022 and approved it 365 days later.

## Agamree approval facts

- Brand name: Agamree
- Active ingredient: vamorolone
- Sponsor: Catalyst Pharms
- Application: NDA 215239
- FDA approval date: October 26, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: October 26, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 365 days
- Dosage form: Suspension
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
