# FDA novel drug approvals in 1997

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 43 novel drugs in 1997: 38 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 5 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

11 of the 43 (26%) had priority review, 7 had orphan drug designation, 2 were accelerated approvals and 0 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 408 days (about 13.4 months).
