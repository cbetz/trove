Zaynich (cefepime and zidebactam) FDA approval

Wockhardt Bio AG · NDA 220787 · approved May 29, 2026

The FDA approved Zaynich (cefepime and zidebactam) on May 29, 2026, under NDA 220787. The sponsor is Wockhardt Bio AG. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat complicated urinary tract infections, including pyelonephritis, caused by designated susceptible microorganisms.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review for the Zaynich application. Whether Zaynich had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
