# Inpefa (sotagliflozin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Inpefa (sotagliflozin) on May 26, 2023, under NDA 216203. The sponsor is Lexicon Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat heart failure.”

FDA reviewed Inpefa on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on May 27, 2022 and approved it 364 days later.

## Inpefa approval facts

- Brand name: Inpefa
- Active ingredient: sotagliflozin
- Sponsor: Lexicon Pharms INC
- Application: NDA 216203
- FDA approval date: May 26, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: May 27, 2022
- Receipt to approval: 364 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
