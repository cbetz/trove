# Voceryq (doruxapapogene ralaplasmid and lalikinogene sifuplasmid-gxfd) FDA approval

The FDA approved Voceryq (doruxapapogene ralaplasmid and lalikinogene sifuplasmid-gxfd) on October 30, 2026. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125825; the manufacturer is Alliance Medical Products Inc. dba Siegfried (AMP), Irvine, CA. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “To reduce the frequency of surgical papilloma excisions in adults with Recurrent Respiratory Papillomatosis (RRP).”
