FDA novel drug approvals in 2022

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 2022.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 37 novel drugs in 2022: 22 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 15 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

24 of the 37 (65%) had priority review, 20 had orphan drug designation, 6 were accelerated approvals, 13 had breakthrough therapy designation and 12 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 335 days (about 11.0 months).

FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) separately approved 5 cell and gene therapy products in 2022, listed in the second table.

What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
