# Nemluvio (nemolizumab-ilto) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nemluvio (nemolizumab-ilto) on August 12, 2024, under BLA 761390. The sponsor is Galderma Labs LP. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat prurigo nodularis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and breakthrough therapy designation for Nemluvio. FDA received the application on December 12, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.

## Nemluvio approval facts

- Brand name: Nemluvio
- Active ingredient: nemolizumab-ilto
- Sponsor: Galderma Labs LP
- Application: BLA 761390
- FDA approval date: August 12, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: December 12, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 244 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Subcutaneous
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
