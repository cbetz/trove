# Azstarys (serdexmethylphenidate anddexmethylphenidate) FDA approval

The FDA approved Azstarys (serdexmethylphenidate anddexmethylphenidate) on March 2, 2021, under NDA 212994. The sponsor is Commave Therap. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat attention deficit hyperactivity disorder.”

FDA reviewed Azstarys on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 2, 2020 and approved it 365 days later.

- Brand name: Azstarys
