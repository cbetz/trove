# Fabhalta (iptacopan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Fabhalta (iptacopan) on December 5, 2023, under NDA 218276. The sponsor is Novartis. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher), orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Fabhalta. FDA received the application on April 5, 2023 and approved it 244 days later.

## Fabhalta approval facts

- Brand name: Fabhalta
- Active ingredient: iptacopan
- Sponsor: Novartis
- Application: NDA 218276
- FDA approval date: December 5, 2023
- Approval year: 2023
- FDA received application: April 5, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 244 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority (used priority review voucher)
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: No
- Priority review voucher redeemed: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
