# Loqtorzi (toripalimab-tpzi) FDA approval

The FDA approved Loqtorzi (toripalimab-tpzi) on October 27, 2023, under BLA 761240. The sponsor is Coherus Biosciences INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat recurrent or metastatic nasopharyngeal carcinoma when used together with or following other therapies.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Loqtorzi. FDA received the application on August 31, 2021 and approved it 787 days later.
