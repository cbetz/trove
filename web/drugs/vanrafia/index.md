# Vanrafia (atrasentan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vanrafia (atrasentan) on April 2, 2025, under NDA 219208. The sponsor is Novartis Pharmaceuticals Corporation. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce proteinuria in adults with primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy at risk of rapid disease progression.”

FDA reviewed Vanrafia on the standard review clock and recorded accelerated approval at original approval. FDA received the application on April 2, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.
