# Roctavian (valoctocogene roxaparvovec-rvox) FDA approval

The FDA approved Roctavian (valoctocogene roxaparvovec-rvox) on June 30, 2023. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125720; the manufacturer is BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “Indicated for the treatment of adults with severe hemophilia A (congenital factor VIII deficiency with factor VIII activity <1 IU/dL) without pre-existing antibodies to adeno-associated virus serotype 5 detected by an FDA-approved test.”
