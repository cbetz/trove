# Brenzavvy (bexagliflozin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Brenzavvy (bexagliflozin) on January 20, 2023, under NDA 214373. The sponsor is Theracosbio. FDA lists the approved use as: “To improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus as an adjunct to diet and exercise.”

FDA reviewed Brenzavvy on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 22, 2021 and approved it 455 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Brenzavvy in the established pharmacologic class: Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 Inhibitor.
