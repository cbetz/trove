# Lifyorli (relacorilant) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lifyorli (relacorilant) on March 25, 2026, under NDA 220641. The sponsor is Corcept Therap. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer after one to three prior systemic treatment regimens, at least one of which included bevacizumab.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Lifyorli application. Whether Lifyorli had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
