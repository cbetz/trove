# Vizz (aceclidine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vizz (aceclidine) on July 31, 2025, under NDA 218585. The sponsor is LENZ Therapeutics, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat presbyopia.”

FDA reviewed Vizz on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on August 8, 2024 and approved it 357 days later.

## Vizz approval facts

- Brand name: Vizz

- Sponsor: LENZ Therapeutics, Inc.
- Application: NDA 218585

- Receipt to approval: 357 days
- Dosage form: Solution/Drops

- Review designation: Standard

- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
