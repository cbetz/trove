# FDA novel drug approvals in 2012

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 39 novel drugs in 2012: 32 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 7 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

16 of the 39 (41%) had priority review, 13 had orphan drug designation, 4 were accelerated approvals, 0 had breakthrough therapy designation and 16 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 304 days (about 10.0 months).
