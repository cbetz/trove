# Xolremdi (mavorixafor) FDA approval

The FDA approved Xolremdi (mavorixafor) on April 26, 2024, under NDA 218709. The sponsor is X4 Pharms. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat WHIM syndrome (warts, hypogammaglobulinemia, infections and myelokathexis).”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Xolremdi. FDA received the application on August 31, 2023 and approved it 239 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Xolremdi in the established pharmacologic class: CXC Chemokine Receptor 4 Antagonist.
