# Aucatzyl (obecabtagene autoleucel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Aucatzyl (obecabtagene autoleucel) on November 8, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125813; the manufacturer is Autolus Limited. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For the treatment of adults with relapsed or refractory B-cell precursor acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL).”

## Aucatzyl approval facts

- Brand name: Aucatzyl

- Manufacturer: Autolus Limited
