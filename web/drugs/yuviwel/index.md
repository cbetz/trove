# Yuviwel (navepegritide) FDA approval

The FDA approved Yuviwel (navepegritide) on February 27, 2026, under NDA 219164. The sponsor is Ascendis. FDA lists the approved use as: “To increase linear growth in pediatric patients 2 years and older with achondroplasia with open epiphyses.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists priority review and orphan status for the Yuviwel application. Whether Yuviwel had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
