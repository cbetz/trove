# Bylvay (odevixibat) FDA approval

The FDA approved Bylvay (odevixibat) on July 20, 2021, under NDA 215498. The sponsor is Ipsen. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat pruritus.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Bylvay. FDA received the application on November 20, 2020 and approved it 242 days later.

## Bylvay approval facts

- Brand name: Bylvay
- Active ingredient: odevixibat
- Sponsor: Ipsen
- Application: NDA 215498
- FDA approval date: July 20, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: November 20, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 242 days
- Dosage form: Capsule
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Priority review voucher issued: Yes (rare pediatric disease)
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
