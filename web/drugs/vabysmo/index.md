# Vabysmo (faricimab-svoa) FDA approval

The FDA approved Vabysmo (faricimab-svoa) on January 28, 2022, under BLA 761235. The sponsor is Genentech INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat neovascular (wet) aged-related macular degeneration and diabetic macular edema.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) for Vabysmo. FDA received the application on May 28, 2021 and approved it 245 days later.
