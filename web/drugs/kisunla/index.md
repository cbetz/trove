# Kisunla (donanemab-azbt) FDA approval

The FDA approved Kisunla (donanemab-azbt) on July 2, 2024, under BLA 761248. The sponsor is Eli Lilly CO. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat Alzheimer’s disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Kisunla. FDA received the application on May 18, 2022 and approved it 776 days later.

## Kisunla approval facts

- Brand name: Kisunla

- Sponsor: Eli Lilly CO
- Application: BLA 761248
- FDA approval date: July 2, 2024
- Approval year: 2024

- Receipt to approval: 776 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Intravenous
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
