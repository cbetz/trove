# FDA novel drug approvals in 1992

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 29 novel drugs in 1992: 27 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 2 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

11 of the 29 (38%) had priority review, 8 had orphan drug designation and 1 were accelerated approvals. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 791 days (about 26.0 months).
