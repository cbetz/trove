# Elrexfio (elranatamab-bcmm) FDA approval

The FDA approved Elrexfio (elranatamab-bcmm) on August 14, 2023, under BLA 761345. The sponsor is Pfizer INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least four prior lines of therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Elrexfio. FDA received the application on December 19, 2022 and approved it 238 days later.
