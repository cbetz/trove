# Lazcluze (lazertinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Lazcluze (lazertinib) on August 19, 2024, under NDA 219008. The sponsor is Janssen Biotech. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review for Lazcluze. FDA received the application on December 20, 2023 and approved it 243 days later.

## Lazcluze approval facts

- Brand name: Lazcluze
- Active ingredient: lazertinib
- Sponsor: Janssen Biotech
- Application: NDA 219008
- FDA approval date: August 19, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: December 20, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 243 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
