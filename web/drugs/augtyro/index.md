# Augtyro (repotrectinib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Augtyro (repotrectinib) on November 15, 2023, under NDA 218213. The sponsor is Bristol. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Augtyro. FDA received the application on March 27, 2023 and approved it 233 days later.

## Augtyro approval facts

- Brand name: Augtyro
- Active ingredient: repotrectinib

- FDA approval date: November 15, 2023

- Receipt to approval: 233 days
- Dosage form: Capsule

- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
