# Terlivaz (terlipressin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Terlivaz (terlipressin) on September 14, 2022, under NDA 022231. The sponsor is Mallinckrodt Ireland. FDA lists the approved use as: “To improve kidney function in adults with hepatorenal syndrome with rapid reduction in kidney function.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and fast track designation for Terlivaz. FDA received the application on May 4, 2009 and approved it 4,881 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Terlivaz in the established pharmacologic class: Vasopressin Receptor Agonist.
