# Rivfloza (nedosiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rivfloza (nedosiran) on September 29, 2023, under NDA 215842. The sponsor is Novo. FDA lists the approved use as: “To lower urinary oxalate levels in patients 9 years and older with primary hyperoxaluria type 1 and relatively preserved kidney function.”

FDA reviewed Rivfloza on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 30, 2022 and approved it 364 days later.
