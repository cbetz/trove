# Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nexviazyme (avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt) on August 6, 2021, under BLA 761194. The sponsor is Genzyme CORP. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat late-onset Pompe disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Nexviazyme. FDA received the application on September 18, 2020 and approved it 322 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Nexviazyme in the established pharmacologic class: Hydrolytic Lysosomal Glycogen-specific Enzyme.
