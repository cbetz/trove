# Amvuttra (vutrisiran) FDA approval

The FDA approved Amvuttra (vutrisiran) on June 13, 2022, under NDA 215515. The sponsor is Alnylam Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat polyneuropathy of hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis.”

FDA reviewed Amvuttra on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on April 14, 2021 and approved it 425 days later.

## Amvuttra approval facts

- Brand name: Amvuttra
- Active ingredient: vutrisiran
- Sponsor: Alnylam Pharms INC

- FDA approval date: June 13, 2022
- Approval year: 2022

- Receipt to approval: 425 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Subcutaneous
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
