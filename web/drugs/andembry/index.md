# Andembry (garadacimab-gxii) FDA approval

The FDA approved Andembry (garadacimab-gxii) on June 16, 2025, under BLA 761367. The sponsor is CSL Behring, LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema.”

FDA reviewed Andembry on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on October 13, 2023 and approved it 612 days later.

## Andembry approval facts

- Brand name: Andembry
- Active ingredient: garadacimab-gxii
- Sponsor: CSL Behring, LLC

- FDA approval date: June 16, 2025
- Approval year: 2025
- FDA received application: October 13, 2023
- Receipt to approval: 612 days
- Dosage form: Injectable
- Route of administration: Subcutaneous
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
