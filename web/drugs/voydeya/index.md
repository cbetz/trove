# Voydeya (danicopan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Voydeya (danicopan) on March 29, 2024, under NDA 218037. The sponsor is Alexion Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat extravascular hemolysis with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria.”

FDA reviewed Voydeya on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 30, 2023 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Voydeya in the established pharmacologic class: Complement Factor D Inhibitor.
