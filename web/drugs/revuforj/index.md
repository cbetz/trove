# Revuforj (revumenib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Revuforj (revumenib) on November 15, 2024, under NDA 218944. The sponsor is Syndax. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsed or refractory acute leukemia.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation, breakthrough therapy designation and fast track designation for Revuforj. FDA received the application on January 26, 2024 and approved it 294 days later.

## Revuforj approval facts

- Brand name: Revuforj
- Active ingredient: revumenib
- Sponsor: Syndax
- Application: NDA 218944
- FDA approval date: November 15, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA received application: January 26, 2024
- Receipt to approval: 294 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: Yes
- Fast track designation: Yes
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
