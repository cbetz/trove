# Genglycos (pariglasgene brecaparvovec-opnr) FDA approval

The FDA approved Genglycos (pariglasgene brecaparvovec-opnr) on August 19, 2026. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125858; the manufacturer is Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc.

## Genglycos approval facts

- Brand name: Genglycos
- Active ingredient: pariglasgene brecaparvovec-opnr
- Manufacturer: Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc
- Application: BLA 125858
- FDA approval date: August 19, 2026
- Approval year: 2026
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Genglycos is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: Indicated to reduce daily cornstarch intake as an adjunct to nutritional management in adult and pediatric patients 8 years of age and older with glycogen storage disease type Ia (GSDIa). This indication is approved under accelerated approval based on reduction in daily cornstarch intake. Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification of clinical benefit in confirmatory trial(s)
