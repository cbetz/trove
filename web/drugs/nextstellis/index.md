# Nextstellis (drospirenone and estetrol) FDA approval

The FDA approved Nextstellis (drospirenone and estetrol) on April 15, 2021, under NDA 214154. The sponsor is Mayne Pharma. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent pregnancy.”

FDA reviewed Nextstellis on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on April 15, 2020 and approved it 365 days later.

## Nextstellis approval facts

- Brand name: Nextstellis
- Active ingredient: drospirenone and estetrol
- Sponsor: Mayne Pharma
- Application: NDA 214154
- FDA approval date: April 15, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: April 15, 2020
- Receipt to approval: 365 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity and Type 4 - New Combination
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
