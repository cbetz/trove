# Rybrevant (amivantamab-vmjw) FDA approval

The FDA approved Rybrevant (amivantamab-vmjw) on May 21, 2021, under BLA 761210. The sponsor is Janssen Biotech. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat a subset of non-small cell lung cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Rybrevant. FDA received the application on November 24, 2020 and approved it 178 days later.
