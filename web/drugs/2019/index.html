FDA novel drug approvals in 2019

Every new molecular entity and new biologic approved by FDA's CDER in calendar year 2019.

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 48 novel drugs in 2019: 38 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 10 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

31 of the 48 (65%) had priority review, 21 had orphan drug designation, 9 were accelerated approvals, 13 had breakthrough therapy designation and 17 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 244 days (about 8.0 months).

What counts as a novel drug approval

FDA uses "novel" for drugs never before approved or marketed in the United States. This list is CDER's count of new molecular entities and new therapeutic biologics. It leaves out generics, biosimilars, new formulations and new indications of drugs already approved, and products licensed by CBER such as vaccines, blood products and cell and gene therapies.
