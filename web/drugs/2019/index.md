# FDA novel drug approvals in 2019

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 48 novel drugs in 2019: 38 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 10 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

31 of the 48 (65%) had priority review, 21 had orphan drug designation, 9 were accelerated approvals, 13 had breakthrough therapy designation and 17 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 244 days (about 8.0 months).
