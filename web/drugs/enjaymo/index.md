# Enjaymo (sutimlimab-jome) FDA approval

The FDA approved Enjaymo (sutimlimab-jome) on February 4, 2022, under BLA 761164. The sponsor is Recordati Rare Diseases, INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To decrease the need for red blood cell transfusion due to hemolysis in cold agglutinin disease.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Enjaymo. FDA received the application on March 13, 2020 and approved it 693 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Enjaymo in the established pharmacologic class: Classical Complement Pathway Inhibitor.
