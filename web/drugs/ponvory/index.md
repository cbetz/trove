# Ponvory (ponesimod) FDA approval

The FDA approved Ponvory (ponesimod) on March 18, 2021, under NDA 213498. The sponsor is Vanda Pharms INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis.”

FDA reviewed Ponvory on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 18, 2020 and approved it 365 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Ponvory in the established pharmacologic class: Sphingosine 1-phosphate Receptor Modulator.
