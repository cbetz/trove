# FDA novel drug approvals in 2000

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 29 novel drugs in 2000: 25 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 4 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

9 of the 29 (31%) had priority review, 3 had orphan drug designation, 2 were accelerated approvals and 2 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 476 days (about 15.6 months).
