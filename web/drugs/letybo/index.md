# Letybo (letibotulinumtoxinA-wlbg) FDA approval

The FDA approved Letybo (letibotulinumtoxinA-wlbg) on February 29, 2024, under BLA 761225. The sponsor is Hugel INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To temporarily improve the appearance of moderate-to-severe glabellar lines.”

FDA reviewed Letybo on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on March 31, 2021 and approved it 1,065 days later.
