# Jemperli (dostarlimab-gxly) FDA approval

The FDA approved Jemperli (dostarlimab-gxly) on April 22, 2021, under BLA 761174. The sponsor is Glaxosmithkline. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat endometrial cancer.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, accelerated approval and breakthrough therapy designation for Jemperli. FDA received the application on December 19, 2019 and approved it 490 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Jemperli in the established pharmacologic class: Programmed Death Receptor-1 Blocking Antibody.
