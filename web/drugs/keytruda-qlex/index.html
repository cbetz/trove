Keytruda Qlex (pembrolizumab and berahyaluronidase alfa-pmph) FDA approval

Merck Sharp and Dohme, LLC. · BLA 761467 · approved Sep 19, 2025

The FDA approved Keytruda Qlex (pembrolizumab and berahyaluronidase alfa-pmph) on September 19, 2025, under BLA 761467. The sponsor is Merck Sharp and Dohme, LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat adult and pediatric (12 years and older) solid tumor indications approved for the intravenous formulation of pembrolizumab.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review (used priority review voucher) and accelerated approval (indication [GG] only) for Keytruda Qlex. FDA received the application on January 23, 2025 and approved it 239 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Keytruda Qlex in the established pharmacologic classes: Endoglycosidase and Programmed Death Receptor-1 Blocking Antibody.
