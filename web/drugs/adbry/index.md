# Adbry (tralokinumab-ldrm) FDA approval

The FDA approved Adbry (tralokinumab-ldrm) on December 27, 2021, under BLA 761180. The sponsor is Leo Pharma AS. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis.”

FDA reviewed Adbry on the standard review clock. Its records show no orphan drug, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on April 27, 2020 and approved it 609 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Adbry in the established pharmacologic class: Interleukin-13 Antagonist.
