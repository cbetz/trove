# Tauklarify (florquinitau F 18) FDA approval

The FDA approved Tauklarify (florquinitau F 18) on August 13, 2026, under NDA 220496. The sponsor is Cerveau Lantheus. FDA lists the approved use as: “To be used for positron emission tomography of the brain in adults with cognitive impairment who are being evaluated for Alzheimer disease to identify patients with tau neurofibrillary tangle pathology.”

FDA's Drugs@FDA record lists standard review for the Tauklarify application. Whether Tauklarify had accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy or fast track designation is not recorded here yet: FDA's compilation of novel approvals does not cover 2026.
