# Beqvez (fidanacogene elaparvovec-dzkt) FDA approval

The FDA approved Beqvez (fidanacogene elaparvovec-dzkt) on April 25, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125786; the manufacturer is Pfizer, Inc.

## Beqvez approval facts

- Brand name: Beqvez
- Active ingredient: fidanacogene elaparvovec-dzkt
- Manufacturer: Pfizer, Inc
- Application: BLA 125786
- FDA approval date: April 25, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
- FDA center: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research)

## What Beqvez is approved for

FDA's product page gives the indication as: For the treatment of adults with moderate to severe hemophilia B (congenital factor IX deficiency) who: Currently use factor IX prophylaxis therapy, or Have current or historical life-threatening hemorrhage, or Have repeated, serious spontaneous bleeding episodes, and, Do not have neutralizing antibodies to adeno-associated virus serotype Rh74var (AAVRh74var) capsid as detected by an FDA-approved test
