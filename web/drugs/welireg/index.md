# Welireg (belzutifan) FDA approval

The FDA approved Welireg (belzutifan) on August 13, 2021, under NDA 215383. The sponsor is Merck Sharp Dohme. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat von Hippel-Lindau disease under certain conditions.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and orphan drug designation for Welireg. FDA received the application on January 15, 2021 and approved it 210 days later.

## Welireg approval facts

- Brand name: Welireg
- Active ingredient: belzutifan
- Sponsor: Merck Sharp Dohme
- Application: NDA 215383
- FDA approval date: August 13, 2021
- Approval year: 2021
- FDA received application: January 15, 2021
- Receipt to approval: 210 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
