# Palsonify (paltusotine) FDA approval

The FDA approved Palsonify (paltusotine) on September 25, 2025, under NDA 219070. The sponsor is Crinetics Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat acromegaly in adults who had an inadequate response to surgery and/or for whom surgery is not an option.”

FDA reviewed Palsonify on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 25, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.
