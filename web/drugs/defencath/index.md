# Defencath (taurolidine, heparin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Defencath (taurolidine, heparin) on November 15, 2023, under NDA 214520. The sponsor is Cormedix. FDA lists the approved use as: “To reduce the incidence of catheter-related bloodstream infections in adults with kidney failure receiving chronic hemodialysis through a central venous catheter.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, fast track designation and qualified infectious disease product designation for Defencath. FDA received the application on June 30, 2020 and approved it 1,233 days later.
