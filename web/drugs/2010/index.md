# FDA novel drug approvals in 2010

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 21 novel drugs in 2010: 13 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 8 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

10 of the 21 (48%) had priority review, 6 had orphan drug designation, 0 were accelerated approvals and 7 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 303 days (about 10.0 months).
