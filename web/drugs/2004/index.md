# FDA novel drug approvals in 2004

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 36 novel drugs in 2004: 28 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 8 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

21 of the 36 (58%) had priority review, 11 had orphan drug designation, 3 were accelerated approvals and 9 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 350 days (about 11.5 months).
