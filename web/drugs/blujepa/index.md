# Blujepa (gepotidacin) FDA approval

The FDA approved Blujepa (gepotidacin) on March 25, 2025, under NDA 218230. The sponsor is GlaxoSmithKline, LLC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat uncomplicated urinary tract infections.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review and qualified infectious disease product designation for Blujepa. FDA received the application on July 26, 2024 and approved it 242 days later.

## Blujepa approval facts

- Brand name: Blujepa
- Active ingredient: gepotidacin
- Sponsor: GlaxoSmithKline, LLC
- Application: NDA 218230
- FDA approval date: March 25, 2025
- Approval year: 2025
- FDA received application: July 26, 2024
- Receipt to approval: 242 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Qualified infectious disease product: Yes
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
