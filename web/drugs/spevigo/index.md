# Spevigo (spesolimab-sbzo) FDA approval

The FDA approved Spevigo (spesolimab-sbzo) on September 1, 2022, under BLA 761244. The sponsor is Boehringer Ingelheim Pharmaceuticals INC. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat generalized pustular psoriasis flares.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and breakthrough therapy designation for Spevigo. FDA received the application on October 1, 2021 and approved it 335 days later.
