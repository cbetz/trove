# Zynteglo (betibeglogene autotemcel) FDA approval

The FDA approved Zynteglo (betibeglogene autotemcel) on August 17, 2022. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125717; the manufacturer is bluebird bio Inc. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For treatment of adult and pediatric patients with ß-thalassemia who require regular red blood cell (RBC) transfusions.”

## Zynteglo approval facts
