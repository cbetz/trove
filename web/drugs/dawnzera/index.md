# Dawnzera (donidalorsen) FDA approval

The FDA approved Dawnzera (donidalorsen) on August 21, 2025, under NDA 219407. The sponsor is Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema.”

FDA reviewed Dawnzera on the standard review clock and recorded orphan drug designation at original approval. FDA received the application on August 21, 2024 and approved it 365 days later.

## Dawnzera approval facts

- Brand name: Dawnzera
- Active ingredient: donidalorsen
- Sponsor: Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc.
- Application: NDA 219407
- FDA approval date: August 21, 2025
- Approval year: 2025
- FDA received application: August 21, 2024
- Receipt to approval: 365 days
- Dosage form: Solution
- Route of administration: Subcutaneous
- Review designation: Standard
- Orphan drug designation: Yes
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
