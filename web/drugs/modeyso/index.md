# Modeyso (dordaviprone) FDA approval

The FDA approved Modeyso (dordaviprone) on August 6, 2025, under NDA 219876. The sponsor is Chimerix, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat diffuse midline glioma harboring an H3 K27M mutation with progressive disease following prior therapy.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review, orphan drug designation and accelerated approval for Modeyso. FDA received the application on December 18, 2024 and approved it 231 days later.

FDA's labeling data puts Modeyso in the established pharmacologic class: Protease Activator.
