# FDA novel drug approvals in 1990

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 24 novel drugs in 1990: 22 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 2 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

13 of the 24 (54%) had priority review and 6 had orphan drug designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 768 days (about 25.2 months).
