# Beyfortus (nirsevimab-alip) FDA approval

The FDA approved Beyfortus (nirsevimab-alip) on July 17, 2023, under BLA 761328. The sponsor is Astrazeneca AB. FDA lists the approved use as: “To prevent respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) lower respiratory tract disease.”

FDA reviewed Beyfortus on the standard review clock and recorded fast track designation at original approval. FDA received the application on September 26, 2022 and approved it 294 days later.
