# FDA novel drug approvals in 2017

FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) approved 46 novel drugs in 2017: 34 under new drug applications (NDAs) and 12 under biologics license applications (BLAs).

29 of the 46 (63%) had priority review, 18 had orphan drug designation, 6 were accelerated approvals, 16 had breakthrough therapy designation and 17 had fast track designation. The median time from FDA's receipt of the application to approval was 244 days (about 8.0 months).
