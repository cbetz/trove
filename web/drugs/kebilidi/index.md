# Kebilidi (eladocagene exuparvovec-tneq) FDA approval

The FDA approved Kebilidi (eladocagene exuparvovec-tneq) on November 13, 2024. It is licensed by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) under BLA 125722; the manufacturer is PTC Therapeutics. FDA's product page gives the indication as: “For the treatment of adult and pediatric patients with aromatic L amino acid decarboxylase (AADC) deficiency.”

## Kebilidi approval facts

- Brand name: Kebilidi
- Active ingredient: eladocagene exuparvovec-tneq
- Manufacturer: PTC Therapeutics
- Application: BLA 125722
- FDA approval date: November 13, 2024
- Approval year: 2024
