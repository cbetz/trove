# Brinsupri (brensocatib) FDA approval

The FDA approved Brinsupri (brensocatib) on August 12, 2025, under NDA 217673. The sponsor is Insmed, Inc. FDA lists the approved use as: “To treat non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis.”

At original approval, FDA recorded priority review for Brinsupri. FDA received the application on December 12, 2024 and approved it 243 days later.

## Brinsupri approval facts

- Brand name: Brinsupri
- Active ingredient: brensocatib
- Sponsor: Insmed, Inc.
- Application: NDA 217673
- FDA approval date: August 12, 2025
- Approval year: 2025
- FDA received application: December 12, 2024
- Receipt to approval: 243 days
- Dosage form: Tablet
- Route of administration: Oral
- Review designation: Priority
- Orphan drug designation: No
- Accelerated approval: No
- Breakthrough therapy designation: No
- Fast track designation: No
- Submission classification: Type 1 - New Molecular Entity
- FDA center: CDER (Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)
