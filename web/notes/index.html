Hospital charity care: the IRS figure and the CMS figure, side by side

Nonprofit hospitals publish a financial-assistance figure on IRS Form 990 Schedule H (line 7a) and a charity-care figure on the CMS cost report (Worksheet S-10, line 23). The two lines are defined differently, so they can differ when both are filed correctly. This note sets them side by side for 493 hospitals in 48 states, walks through Yale New Haven Hospital's two filings line by line, and lists what the components show, and do not show, for the 25 largest differences.

The tools behind them

  • Hospital reporting — a lookup of 2,099 matched nonprofit hospital systems with the IRS and CMS figures side by side, plus a ranked index of 493 hospitals.
  • FDA drug approvals — 331 novel approvals, 2021–2026, each with its approval package.
  • Claude Code skills — ask questions over both datasets in plain English.
  • Docs — plain-language explainers for each dataset.
