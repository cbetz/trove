Docs

Plain-language explainers for the datasets trove indexes.

Three of the most widely cited public-domain datasets in U.S. healthcare are also three of the least approachable. CMS Medicare Cost Reports are shipped as long CSVs with one row per worksheet cell, millions of rows per year. IRS Form 990 Schedule H is inside XML files in bulk ZIPs. FDA approval packages are scattered across hundreds of PDF directories on accessdata.fda.gov. The data is public; learning what's in it is a project.

These pages explain what each dataset is, what's in it, and how to use it — in plain language, with citations.

Datasets

CMS

What is HCRIS?

CMS Medicare Cost Reports — the financial filing every Medicare-participating hospital submits annually on form 2552-10. Covers Worksheet S-10 (charity care, line 5, uncompensated care), how the fiscal-year files are organized, and why a blank is not a zero.

 IRS

What is IRS Form 990 Schedule H?

The schedule nonprofit hospitals file with their annual Form 990. Covers Part I line 7a (financial assistance at cost) and its three dollar columns, the other community benefit lines, and how line 7a sits next to the CMS charity-care line.

 FDA

What is an FDA novel drug approval?

The annual curated list of meaningful first-time drug approvals (NMEs and novel BLAs), and the approval package each one ships with — medical, statistical, pharmacology, and chemistry reviews.

 FDA

What is a new molecular entity (NME)?

FDA's definition, what "active moiety" means, how an NME differs from a new chemical entity (NCE) and from a new biologic, and how many FDA approves each year.

 FDA

What is an FDA approval package?

The approval letter, the label and FDA's review documents: what each contains, what Summary Basis of Approval means, and how to find them on Drugs@FDA.

 FDA

What is an FDA Drug Trials Snapshot?

FDA's short summary of who took part in the trials behind a new drug approval, by sex, race, age and ethnicity. Which drugs have one and how to find it.

Tools

The docs explain the data. The tools let you query it.

  • Browse FDA drug approvals (2021–2026) — 331 approvals (295 CDER NMEs and novel BLAs plus 36 CBER cell & gene therapies), search and detail pages.
  • FDA drug approval statistics, 1985–2025 — novel approvals per year, review designations and review times, with a page for each year's list.
  • FDA's NME compilation as CSV and Parquet — all 1,387 CDER novel approvals since 1985, converted from FDA's Excel file.
  • Hospital charity care: the IRS figure and the CMS figure, side by side — IRS Schedule H line 7a and CMS Worksheet S-10 line 23 for 2,099 matched nonprofit hospital systems, tax year 2022, with the components from each filing that show what is in the difference.
  • Install the Claude Code skills — natural-language query over both datasets.
  • Read the notes — write-ups from the published tables.
  • Source on GitHub — parsers, build scripts and the data files, MIT-licensed.
