# Community Benefit Cross-Form Dataset — TY2022 (CMS HCRIS S-10 × IRS 990 Schedule H)

Generated by `scripts/build_gap_index.py` from `community_benefit_gap_2022.csv` — every number below is computed from that file; do not hand-edit.

## What changed in this version

- **Missing is no longer published as $0.** CMS's numeric file never stores a zero, so an absent Worksheet S-10 line is *blank or zero*, not a reported $0. Earlier versions summed an all-missing S-10 into `0`; those cells are now empty and the difference is empty with them.
- **The IRS 2023 e-file release is included.** It carries most calendar-year filers' TY2022 returns and was never ingested before. Releases 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 are all read, ZIP by ZIP, without relying on the IRS index to locate returns.
- **Cost reports are matched to the 990's own fiscal period.** CMS files a cost report under the federal fiscal year in which its period *begins*, so the FY2023 file alone holds the right year only for October–December fiscal-year starts. The FY2022 and FY2023 files are now both loaded and, per CCN, the report ending closest to the 990's tax-period end is used. Worksheet S-10 is `S100000` for periods beginning before 2022-10-01 and `S100001` (Part I) after; the line and column numbers used are the same in both.
- **Components are published**, not just two totals: Schedule H line 7a columns (c), (d), (e) and line 7b; S-10 line 23 columns 1–3, lines 20–22, and line 5; report status, period dates, CCNs, and the IRS object id of the exact return.

## The funnel — read this first

- **2,099 matched systems** (one row per EIN with a TY2022 Schedule H and at least one crosswalked CCN with a cost report).
- **1,904 are computable** — both S-10 line 23 col 3 and Schedule H 7a col (e) are present. Of the other 195: 159 have no S-10 charity-care figure (69 filed no Worksheet S-10 at all on at least one CCN; 21 are multi-CCN systems where only some CCNs report it — see `hcris_charity_care_cost_reporting_ccns`) and 53 have a blank Schedule H 7a (some rows are both).
- **1,797 of the computable are period-aligned** — every CCN under the EIN has a cost report ending within 1 calendar month of the 990's tax-period end. 120 of all matched rows are not aligned (no cost report for that period in the FY2022/FY2023 files, or a multi-CCN system with a facility on a different fiscal year); they stay in the file with `period_aligned = False`.
- **1,141 of those also have both figures ≥ $500K** — a noise floor, not a comparability test. (A negative figure does not pass it.)
- **493 are in the [ranked index](charity_care_gap_index_2022.md)** — one CCN with a cost report, period-aligned, both figures ≥ $1M, total expenses within 5% — across 48 states; **107** of them differ by more than 50%.

## Method

For every EIN that appears in both the CBI CCN↔EIN crosswalk and the TY2022 Schedule H feed, the return of record is the full-year return with the latest period end, then the latest e-file timestamp (so an amendment supersedes the original). For each of the EIN's CCNs, the cost report whose period end is closest to that return's tax-period end is selected from the FY2022 and FY2023 HCRIS files; S-10 figures are summed across the EIN's CCNs.

`charity_gap = S-10 line 23 col 3 − Schedule H Part I line 7a col (e)` (net basis)

`charity_gap_gross = S-10 line 23 col 3 − Schedule H Part I line 7a col (c)` (gross basis)

Positive → the S-10 figure is larger. Both are empty when either side is. 469 matched rows report direct offsetting revenue on line 7a (column d), which is exactly the part of a net-basis difference that the gross basis removes.

## How to read a difference

Schedule H Part I line 7a is financial assistance at cost: column (c) total community benefit expense, column (d) direct offsetting revenue, column (e) net = (c) − (d). Worksheet S-10 line 23 column 3 is the cost of charity care after patient payments: uninsured patients (column 1) plus insured patients (column 2). The two lines are defined differently, so they can differ even when both are filed correctly. The components in the file show where to look:

- **Medicaid supplemental payments and provider taxes.** S-10 line 5 (`hcris_medicaid_dsh_supplemental`) is Medicaid DSH/supplemental payments net of provider taxes; it is present on 1,136 matched rows and negative on 87. It is not part of S-10 line 23. The Schedule H instructions (Worksheet 1, line 4) let a filer include provider taxes in line 7a cost under a stated condition, so where line 5 is negative it is worth comparing with the difference.
- **Offsetting revenue.** Schedule H 7a column (e) is net of direct offsetting revenue (column d); S-10 line 23 is net only of patient payments. Compare `charity_gap_gross` with `charity_gap`.
- **Insured patients.** S-10 line 23 column 2 (`hcris_charity_care_cost_insured`) counts deductibles and coinsurance written off for insured patients; column 1 (`hcris_charity_care_cost_uninsured`) is uninsured patients only. Compare the Schedule H figure with each.
- **Costing method.** S-10 applies the cost-to-charge ratio from the cost report; the Schedule H instructions let a filer use the ratio from their Worksheet 2 or a cost accounting method it considers more accurate.
- **Return arithmetic.** `sched_h_fa_7a_consistent` is False where the return's own line 7a does not satisfy (c) − (d) = (e) (40 matched rows); on those rows the difference rests on a figure that cannot be read at face value.

S-10 charity care is **all-payer**, so a difference where the Schedule H figure is the larger one is not explained by low Medicare volume. **Missing is not zero:** 159 matched rows have no S-10 line 23 figure (69 of them with no Worksheet S-10 at all on at least one CCN), including 32 of the 44 Maryland rows. Those rows are empty here, never $0, and no difference is computed for them.

## Home-county SVI columns (proxy)

Each row carries `svi_overall_pct` (0-100) plus four sub-theme percentiles. These are county-level CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index 2022 ranks for the home county of each facility, taken as the median across a system's facilities. This is a **home-county proxy**, not a catchment-area measure; do not use it to infer service-area need or the adequacy of charity care. CDC SVI is US government work, public domain.

## Coverage and caveats

- **Sources and vintage.** CMS HCRIS Hospital 2552-10 FY2022 and FY2023 files, CMS extract generated 2026-10-02, posted 2026-10-05, latest cost-report processing date 2026-09-30 (extract date: the timestamps of the files inside the CMS ZIPs; posted date: the Last-Modified date of the CMS download). The FY2024 file was checked and adds no period-matched report. IRS e-file releases 2023–2026; returns used, by release: 2023: 763, 2024: 1,294, 2025: 42.
- **Cost report status** of the reports used (a multi-CCN row counts once per distinct status): settled without audit: 1,483, as submitted: 393, amended: 235, settled with audit: 98, reopened: 82. As-submitted and amended reports can still change.
- **Alignment is end-date based** (within 1 calendar month). In the index the total-expense test additionally screens out stub periods and scope mismatches; the full file does not apply it.
- **Multi-facility EINs.** 218 rows roll more than one CCN into one EIN; the row is named for the largest facility. System-level sums can hide facility-level differences. `hcris_charity_care_cost` is empty if any CCN lacks line 23.
- **Crosswalk CCNs with no cost report.** `ccn_count` counts the EIN's CCNs that have a cost report in the FY2022/FY2023 files; `crosswalk_ccn_count` counts the CCNs the crosswalk lists. They differ on 180 rows (116 of them with `ccn_count` = 1). S-10 figures are summed over the CCNs that have a report, so a CCN with no report at all contributes nothing and does not empty the total. Across all matched EINs the crosswalk lists 245 CCNs that have no cost report in the FY2022 or FY2023 file; none of them appears in the FY2024 file either, which is consistent with retired or merged provider numbers. Either way, `ccn_count` = 1 means "one CCN with a cost report", not "the crosswalk lists one facility".
- **Crosswalk.** The CBI CCN↔EIN crosswalk is frozen at its December 2024 vintage (2,385 EINs). Hospitals that are for-profit, government-owned without a 990, covered only by a group return under another EIN, or missing from CBI are not matched.
- **Short-year returns.** Where an EIN filed both a full-year and a short-year return on TY2022 forms, the full-year return is used.
- **Parser validation.** The Yale New Haven Hospital row (EIN 060646652, CCN 070022, period ending 2023-09-30) is pinned in the test suite against both source filings to the dollar.
